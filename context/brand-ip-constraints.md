# Brand IP Constraints

This file documents hard constraints on language and terminology that arise from brand sponsorship, intellectual property licensing, and trademark restrictions. Constraints here are NOT style preferences, they are legal-compliance requirements. Violations create real legal exposure for ProSoccer.

## FIFA World Cup Terminology

Adidas is the official sponsor and commercial licensee for FIFA-related terminology in soccer retail contexts.

### Restricted terms (forbidden on non-Adidas pages)

- "World Cup"
- "FIFA World Cup"
- "FIFA World Cup 2026" (or any year)
- "WC" (as abbreviation for World Cup)
- "FIFA" (when used in a commercial promotional context)
- Any clear variation of the above

### Where restricted terms ARE allowed

- Adidas-branded collection pages (e.g., `/collections/adidas-2026-fifa-world-cup-soccer-jerseys-gear`)
- Adidas product pages
- National team collection pages where the team wears Adidas (Argentina, Mexico, Germany, Spain, Italy, Belgium, Japan, etc., verify per-team during topic research)
- Adidas-only national team kit product pages

### Where restricted terms are FORBIDDEN

- Brand-agnostic umbrella collections (e.g., `/collections/2026-national-team-soccer-fan-gear`, covers all federations regardless of brand)
- Nike-branded collection pages
- Puma-branded collection pages
- Other non-Adidas brand collections
- National team collection pages where the team wears non-Adidas brand (Brazil/Nike, France/Nike, USMNT/Nike, England/Nike, Netherlands/Nike, Portugal/Nike, Canada/Nike, Switzerland/Puma, etc., verify per-team during topic research)
- Any product page for a non-Adidas product

### Federation-anchored substitution language

When restricted terms cannot be used, substitute with neutral terminology:

| Restricted | Allowed alternative |
|---|---|
| "2026 World Cup" | "2026 international tournament" / "the 2026 federation cycle" / "the 2026 tournament" |
| "FIFA World Cup 2026" | "the 2026 international football championship" / "the 2026 federation tournament" |
| "World Cup squad" | "Federation roster" / "national team squad" |
| "World Cup kit" | "Federation kit" / "national team kit" / "2026 federation jersey" |
| "WC 2026" | "2026" alone (year reference is fine without FIFA invocation) |

The year "2026" alone is ALWAYS permitted. The constraint is on FIFA-trademarked phrases, not on the calendar year.

### Verification step required in workflow

Before SCRIBE finalizes any brief, perform brand-affiliation classification:

1. Identify page type and primary subject:
   - Brand-specific collection (Adidas-only / Nike-only / Puma-only / other)
   - National team collection (identify which brand makes their kit)
   - Brand-agnostic umbrella collection (covers multiple brands by definition)
   - Product page (identify the product's brand)

2. Apply terminology constraint per classification:
   - Adidas-classified: FIFA terminology family allowed
   - Non-Adidas-classified: FIFA terminology family forbidden, use Federation alternatives

3. Document classification reasoning in the workforce-internal section of the brief.

4. Scan the proposed copy across all six fields (Title, Slug, SEO Meta Title, SEO Meta Description, Short Description, Long Description) plus internal link anchors for restricted-term violations. If any found on a non-Adidas-classified page, flag and rewrite using substitution language.
