### Tiempo Maestro Academy Turf (IQ2388-901) (added 2026-06-29, Batch 5)
- Maestro is NOT new to this silo (Maestro Elite FG/AG already shipped); Maestro-vs-Legend is already codified (Techleather + Maestro360 split plate vs k-leather Legend). The firsts in this SKU are the **Academy tier** and the **first Tiempo Turf** in the workforce.
- **Academy tier = Nike's accessible mid/entry tier** (Nike ladder: Elite > Pro > Academy > Club). Per the brand tier nomenclature (`context/workforce-conventions.md` 'Brand tier nomenclature'), Nike Academy maps to adidas League. **Use the League/Club word band (280 to 340 words)**, NOT the Elite/Pro band: this is an entry-tier SKU.
- Turf (TF): rubber turf outsole for artificial turf; the AG-Pro / FG plate language from the Elite entries does not apply, this is a turf shoe.
- Keep the touch/control Tiempo lane, sized to the entry tier and the turf surface; do not reuse the Elite Maestro "conductor / tempo" hooks already in the log.
