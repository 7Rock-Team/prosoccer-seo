## Silo note: F50 SPARKFUSION is a second silhouette under F50, and it is women's (added 2026-08-25, Batch 15.1)

Everything below this note, and every F50 primary of record, is **F50 Hyperfast**. Sparkfusion is a separate silhouette in the same F50 family and it was invisible to this silo until Batch 15.1.

**Live facts, verified 2026-08-25 against the refreshed sitemap and live product titles.** Twelve live Sparkfusion pages. **All twelve are women's**, confirmed by H1, including the four whose handles carry no `womens` token (`adidas-f50-sparkfusion-{elite,league}-fg-ag-soccer-cleats-chaos-vs-control` and the matching `-trinity-rodman` pair, all four titled `adidas Women's F50 Sparkfusion ...`). This is a live case of the handle-versus-title rule: read from the handle, four of the twelve look unisex.

**Term ownership: INTERIM, not settled.** `adidas f50 sparkfusion`, `f50 sparkfusion`, the women's-qualified variants and the two tier-level terms are ceded to `/collections/adidas-f50` (`collections-master.csv` `ceded_from`, derived into `ceded-terms.csv` 2026-08-25). That collection already carries 8 of the 12 and its H1 names women, which is why it is the least-wrong interim home. It is still a compromise: **the collection does not name the model**, so the Sparkfusion model term is interim-owned rather than owned. Collection creation is Jorge's call (`strategy/sprint-backlog.md` B-MERCH-01); the ownership gap is logged as a B-KW-01 entry in its own right, because interim ownership by a collection that does not name the model is not the same as the term being owned.

**Positioning consequence for this silo.** The F50 speed story in the log below is written to an adult men's or a parent-facing buyer. Sparkfusion pages address **an adult woman buying for herself**, which is a different purchase motivation and not a variant of either. Do not write Sparkfusion copy in the parent-facing junior lane, and do not default it to Tyler. See `strategy/sprint-backlog.md` B-AVATAR-02.

**Competitive context available:** Trinity Rodman is a real signature sibling at both tiers (`...-fg-ag-soccer-cleats-trinity-rodman`, both live and purchasable), so women's-specific competitive framing is supportable where the scrape carries it. Do not invent it where the scrape does not.
