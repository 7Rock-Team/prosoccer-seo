# Homepage Playbook

_Page-type playbook for the homepage at `/` (and any homepage variant like `/pages/home` if Shopify routes one). Read by SCRIBE on every homepage brief, ahead of the six copy-writing principles in `context/03-brand-voice.md`. The playbook governs subject matter (what the page is ABOUT). The six principles govern execution quality (HOW the copy reads)._

## Subject focus

The homepage IS about ProSoccer. This is the one page where store positioning lives. LA presence, Pasadena retail, Irwindale warehouse, 30-year heritage, three retail locations, authentic-curation difference, and the High-Performance Expert wedge are all in scope here. Collection pages and product pages keep the store invisible because the avatar didn't come for the store. The homepage is the inverse: the avatar came to figure out what kind of store this is.

The homepage is also the routing layer. After establishing what kind of store this is, the homepage points the avatar toward the category they actually want (national teams for Carlos, cleats for Tyler, kids' gear for Jennifer, team orders for Mike the Coach).

## Forbidden subjects on the homepage

- Generic e-commerce platitudes. "Shop the best deals," "find what you need," "your one-stop shop for soccer." These were forbidden by `context/03-brand-voice.md` rules anyway. The homepage is positioning, not a tagline contest.
- Specific products in too much detail. The homepage names categories and surfaces drops, but doesn't run product descriptions. Product detail belongs on product pages.
- Long-tail keyword stuffing. The homepage targets brand and category head terms (`pro soccer`, `soccer cleats`, `soccer jerseys`), not long-tail. Long-tail belongs on collection and product pages.
- Marketing-deck language that sounds polished and means nothing. Avatar-anchored specifics beat polished abstractions.

## Required subjects on the homepage

- ProSoccer's positioning. The High-Performance Expert wedge from `context/00-business-overview.md` 'Identity and Positioning'. LA Soccer Specialty as the geographic identity. The four binding positioning claims: High-Performance Expert, Pasadena / Irwindale geographic moat, 30-year heritage, authentic-curation difference.
- Differentiation from competitors. What ProSoccer competes on (specialized expertise, authentic curation, geographic presence, problem-solving) and what ProSoccer chooses NOT to compete on (logistics scale, head-term volume, casual-buyer convenience). Per `context/00-business-overview.md`, this is binding.
- Trust signals tied to the four positioning claims. Heritage (since the 1990s), retail presence (Pasadena and Irwindale), expert curation (we don't carry every SKU; we carry the right SKUs).
- Avatar-anchored hooks for each primary avatar. Carlos (national teams, drops, authenticity). Tyler (performance cleats, pro endorsements, latest releases). Jennifer (youth, fit, safety). Mike the Coach (team orders, bulk, durability). Each avatar gets a clear entry point on the page.
- Current site-wide priorities and catalysts. World Cup 2026, season transitions (back-to-school, summer-club season), major drop windows. Catalyst content rotates seasonally; the playbook frame stays constant.

## Required pre-write research

Lighter than collection-page or product-page research because the topic IS the store and the team already knows the store. Required reads at session start:

- `context/00-business-overview.md` for positioning, the four binding claims, the differentiation language.
- `context/04-customer-avatars.md` for the four avatar pain frames and emotional anchors.
- `context/05-competitors.md` (when populated) for the differentiation language against Soccer.com, Dick's, brand DTCs, and Niky's Sports.
- `strategy/sprint-backlog.md` and `strategy/master-strategy.md` for current site-wide priorities and active catalysts.

Tavily research is rarely needed for a homepage build because the topic is the store and the workforce already owns the source-of-truth on the store. The exception is competitor-positioning research, which RECON runs and feeds in via the consolidated brief flow.

## Field-specific rules

The homepage has different fields from a collection or product page because Shopify's homepage is built from theme sections rather than a single description body. The fields below are the SEO-relevant copy surfaces; theme sections (hero, category grid, testimonial rail, drop carousel) are the visual structure that holds them.

### Title (Storefront Title)

The brand-positioning hero. Names ProSoccer and the wedge. Avatar-search-language preserved.

- `ProSoccer | LA's Soccer Specialty Store Since the 1990s`
- `ProSoccer | The High-Performance Soccer Specialist`

### SEO Meta Title

Brand-first for SERP discovery on brand searches and category head terms. Brand sits in the first 30 characters because brand-search SERPs are how regulars find the site.

- `ProSoccer | Authentic Cleats, Jerseys & Soccer Gear in LA`
- `ProSoccer.com | LA Soccer Specialty Store, Pasadena & Irwindale`

50 to 60 characters.

### SEO Meta Description

Summarizes what the store is and who it serves. The four positioning claims surface here in compressed form. CTA is a category routing CTA, not a generic "shop now."

- `Authentic soccer cleats, jerseys, and gear in LA. Three retail locations, expert fitting, same-day dispatch from Irwindale. Shop national teams, brand catalogs, and the latest drops.`
- `LA's soccer specialty store since the 1990s. Authentic kits, performance cleats, training gear, and team orders, with retail in Pasadena and Irwindale.`

150 to 158 characters. The avatar-anchored hooks (national teams for Carlos, performance for Tyler, retail for Jennifer's in-person fitting, team orders for Mike the Coach) compress into category names.

### Hero copy (above-the-fold positioning hook)

The emotional positioning hook. The first sentence carries who ProSoccer is and what the store stands for. Per `context/03-brand-voice.md` 'Emotional Connection Over Feature Selling', features come in supporting sentences if at all.

40 to 80 words for the hero block (the visible above-fold text on desktop and mobile combined).

This is the homepage's most-read sentence. It either commits to the wedge or it doesn't. "We're a soccer store" is not a wedge; "We've been LA's soccer specialty since the 1990s, and the team behind the counter actually plays the sport" is a wedge.

### Below-fold sections

Three to six theme-rendered sections that route avatars toward the categories they want.

Standard frame:

- **Section 1: Catalyst block.** What's most urgent right now on the site. World Cup 2026 in Wave 1 of 2026; summer-club season in late spring; back-to-school in late summer. The catalyst block routes Carlos and Tyler to drops and Jennifer to youth gear depending on the season.
- **Section 2: National teams entry point.** Carlos's primary route. Names the World Cup and the major federations.
- **Section 3: Cleats and brand catalogs entry point.** Tyler's primary route. Names the brand-tier categories (Adidas Predator, Nike Mercurial, Puma Future).
- **Section 4: Youth and fit entry point.** Jennifer's primary route. Names youth gear, wide-fit cleats, fitting-room access. (This section is the one place the homepage references the Pasadena fitting room as a consumer benefit, because Jennifer's pain frames map directly to in-person fitting.)
- **Section 5: Team orders entry point.** Mike the Coach's primary route. Names bulk pricing, invoicing, club uniforms.
- **Section 6: Trust block.** Heritage, retail locations, the team-behind-the-counter framing. Specifics, not platitudes.

Each below-fold section runs 30 to 60 words of copy plus a category link. The visual section (image, layout, CTA button) is theme work; the copy is SCRIBE work.

## Worked example: Full homepage hero plus below-fold sections

Avatar coverage: Carlos primary (national teams), Tyler primary (cleats and brand catalogs), Jennifer primary (youth and fit), Mike the Coach primary (team orders). The homepage is the only page where all four avatars are primary.
Catalyst block (May 2026): World Cup 2026, six weeks to kickoff, draft kit drops clustered.

```
Storefront Title
ProSoccer | LA's Soccer Specialty Store Since the 1990s

SEO Meta Title
ProSoccer | LA Soccer Specialty, Cleats, Jerseys & Team Gear
[58 chars]

SEO Meta Description
LA's soccer specialty store since the 1990s. Authentic cleats, national team kits, and team orders, with retail in Pasadena and Irwindale and same-day dispatch.
[157 chars]

Hero copy
LA's soccer specialty store since the 1990s. The team behind the counter actually watches the matches, plays Sunday rec, and remembers the year you walked in for your first kit. Authentic gear, expert fitting, three retail locations across LA County.

Below-fold sections:

## Section 1: Catalyst (May 2026)
The 2026 World Cup kicks off June 11 at Estadio Azteca. Mexico, Argentina, Brazil, France, England, USMNT, and every other major federation kit is in stock or on its way. Sign up for the drop list and the kit lands in your inbox the morning Adidas or Nike releases it.

## Section 2: National Teams
Mexico, Argentina, Brazil, France, England, USMNT, USWNT, Italy, Holland, Spain, Germany, and the rest of the World Cup field. Authentic kits direct from the brand, the player edition and the fan cut, sized and customized for the way you actually wear them.

## Section 3: Cleats
Adidas Predator, Nike Mercurial, Puma Future, and the rest of the lineup. Firm ground for natural grass, AG for artificial grass, TF for older astroturf, IC and IN for indoor and futsal. The team behind the counter has played in most of them; ask for the read.

## Section 4: Youth & Fit
Wide-fit cleats, growing-foot sizing, turf-safe plates, and the fitting room in Pasadena that's open until 8 pm on weekdays. Bring the kid. Try the cleat. Walk out knowing it fits before the match.

## Section 5: Team Orders
Club uniforms, training kits, bulk balls, cones, and pinnies. Net-30 invoicing for clubs and schools. Same-day dispatch on stock items from Irwindale. Email the team-orders desk for a quote.

## Section 6: Three Retail Locations Across LA
Pasadena. Irwindale. Plus the warehouse showroom in Irwindale for pickup. Thirty years of stocking the LA soccer community. The store that's been here since the kids who shopped here have brought their own kids in.
```

Annotation:

- The homepage commits to the wedge in the first sentence (hero copy).
- The four avatars each get a routing entry point: Carlos (Sections 1 and 2), Tyler (Section 3), Jennifer (Section 4), Mike the Coach (Section 5).
- The four binding positioning claims surface naturally: High-Performance Expert (Section 3 read), Pasadena / Irwindale geographic moat (Section 4, Section 6), 30-year heritage (hero, Section 6), authentic-curation difference (Section 2 "authentic kits direct from the brand").
- The catalyst (World Cup 2026) routes through Section 1 and surfaces again in Section 2; this rotates seasonally without rebuilding the rest of the page.
- The store IS the topic, but specifics still beat platitudes: "the team behind the counter actually watches the matches" beats "we love soccer."
- Lift test (Gate 9 from `.claude/agents/on-page-seo/agent.md` Section 11): the LA-specific framing, the three-retail-locations specificity, the 1990s heritage, the team-orders Net-30 detail are all anchored to ProSoccer specifically and could not appear on Soccer.com.
- Voice rules: no forbidden words, no em-dashes, contractions used, varied sentence rhythm, specifics throughout.

## How this playbook integrates with the six copy-writing principles

Same integration order as the collection-page and product-page playbooks:

1. Read this playbook. Confirm what the homepage is about.
2. Refresh the four positioning claims from `context/00-business-overview.md` and the four avatar emotional anchors from `context/04-customer-avatars.md`.
3. Apply the field-specific rules above to determine WHAT each field and section is about.
4. Apply the six copy-writing principles from `context/03-brand-voice.md` and `.claude/agents/on-page-seo/agent.md` Section 7 to determine HOW each field reads.
5. Run `voice_check.py` on the staged copy.
6. Self-verify per `.claude/agents/on-page-seo/agent.md` Section 11.

Conflicts between this playbook and a copy-writing principle escalate to ORIN.

## Note on cadence

The homepage is not a Wave 1 priority for the current sprint. The Wave 1 work centers on national-team collection pages (Mexico, Argentina, Brazil, France, USMNT). The homepage gets revisited after Wave 1 ships and once cumulative learnings from the collection-page work are reflected back into the homepage's positioning copy. This playbook is in place so that when the homepage build runs, the workforce isn't starting from a blank slate.
