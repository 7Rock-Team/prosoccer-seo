## EVERGREEN FRAMING NOTES (architectural review surface)

The dispatch spec called for explicit framing notes for cross-category evergreen pattern emergence. Below are the framing decisions made on this brief, what worked, what surfaced as architectural questions, and recommended template refinement candidates.

### Framing decisions

1. **Primary keyword = literal page handle term.** Chose `soccer apparel` (KD 7, 1,900/mo) over `soccer gear` (KD 14, 5,400/mo) despite the 2.8x volume gap because the page handle, H1, breadcrumb, and literal page scope all anchor on "apparel." Mexico v5's cycle-bound year-specificity-inverts-at-collection-head-term rule does NOT apply on evergreen pages; instead, the evergreen analogue is "primary keyword = literal page handle term unless the literal handle term is itself a low-volume long-tail." This framing decision worked because the head term matched the catalog's natural intent.

2. **Body Description substance from catalog data, not external research.** Zero Tavily queries. The Firecrawl scrape surfaced product counts, brand counts, made-for breakdowns, size ladders, club-team and federation rosters that became the H2 body substance. This works for evergreen multi-brand category pages where the catalog IS the topic; this WOULD NOT work for national-team or player collection pages where cultural identity is the substance.

3. **H2 framework: 4 H2s, no contained-catalyst section.** Mexico v5 followed the 5-evergreen-plus-1-catalyst rule; this brief uses 4 evergreen H2s with zero catalyst section because the page is genuinely evergreen and tournament-cycle-independent. The catalyst-section framework doesn't apply on the evergreen surface. The four H2s map to: (1) What's in the catalog (substance), (2) The brand bench (brand-by-brand consideration), (3) Use-case taxonomy (match day / training / off-pitch), (4) Sizing scope (whole-family ladder).

4. **Multi-brand brand-IP classification = brand-agnostic.** This is the first canonical brand-agnostic Tier 2B brief in the workforce's reference set. FIFA terminology family scrubbed throughout; federation-anchored substitution applied. This compliance posture is the inverse of Mexico v5's adidas-licensed permission. **Architectural pattern: brand-agnostic = strictest FIFA compliance.**

5. **Internal link strategy = broader-catalog-destination preference applied to apparel-segment routing.** Selected `/collections/official-licensed-jerseys` (43% of catalog) and `/collections/training-jerseys` (7.6% of catalog) as the two segment-deep-catalog links. Rejected brand-collection routing (adidas / Nike) to preserve multi-brand-neutrality narrative. Rejected nav-redundant routing (/men, /women, /youth-apparel) because the nav menu surfaces those without body Description support.

6. **FAQ SKIP per Refinement 2.** Body H2 substance covers the buyer questions that have signal except cross-brand sizing, which requires empirical Mike-side data. Flagged as future enhancement.

### What worked

- The literal-handle-term primary keyword discipline.
- Catalog-data-as-topic-substance discipline.
- The 4-H2 framework matching multi-brand category page scope.
- The brand-bench narrative as a way to honor brand-neutrality while still naming brands for Rule 4 LLM-discoverability.

Recommend: evergreen pages stay evergreen-linked; cycle-aware routing happens at the umbrella WC26 collection's own promotional surface (banner/UI level), not at the apparel page's body Description.
