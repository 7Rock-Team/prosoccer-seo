## Key findings (sourced)

### 2026 World Cup specifics

- **Opening match:** June 11, 2026 at Estadio Azteca, Mexico City. Mexico vs South Africa, a repeat of the 2010 World Cup opener (16 years on). [losangelesfwc26.com FIFA WC26 schedule reveal; si.com Mexico 2026 schedule article; usatoday.com 2026-04-22]
- **Group A composition:** Mexico, South Africa, South Korea, Czechia (Czechia advanced via European playoffs against Denmark). [si.com schedule article]
- **Stadium history:** Estadio Azteca becomes the first stadium in the world to host three FIFA World Cup opening matches (1970, 1986, 2026). [beINSPORTS USA Facebook; losangelesfwc26.com]
- **Mexico's matches at Azteca:** Up to four group + knockout matches if El Tri tops Group A; up to five total on home soil. [si.com]
- **Final:** July 19, 2026, at MetLife Stadium, New York / New Jersey. [losangelesfwc26.com]

### Squad and coach

- **Head coach:** Javier Aguirre (third World Cup as El Tri manager). [nytimes.com athletic 2026-04-28]
- **Assistant coach:** Rafael Marquez ("El Kaiser," widely regarded as the best defender in Mexican history). [Wikipedia Rafael Marquez]
- **Pre-WC camp:** Began May 6 in Mexico City; FMF released a list of 20 Liga MX-based players, 12 of whom are guaranteed a final-26 spot. The remaining 14 squad slots come from Europe-based and other-league-based players. [nytimes.com athletic 2026-04-28]
- **Confirmed key names:** Edson Alvarez (West Ham, double-pivot anchor), Hirving "Chucky" Lozano (winger), Santiago Gimenez (striker, Feyenoord-to-Milan move), Raul Jimenez (veteran striker), Alexis Vega, Henry Martin, Diego Lainez, Roberto Alvarado, Uriel Antuna. Goalkeeper: Malagon. [espn.com squad page; goal.com 2026 squad article; nytimes.com athletic]
- **Predicted starting XI (4-3-3):** Malagon; Gallardo, Vasquez, Araujo, Sanchez; Alvarez, Mora, Ruiz; Lozano, Jimenez, Gimenez. [goal.com]

### 2026 Home Kit (Adidas)

- **Design:** Pre-Hispanic Aztec-inspired motifs across the chest. Climacool fabric. FMF crest centered traditional. Verde primary. [adidas.com mexico-26-home-jersey product page; goal.com 2026 kits article; tudnfanshop.com authentic home jersey]
- **Design history:** This is reportedly Adidas's second attempt at the 2026 home kit. The first version (Mexican flag colors running down the center) was scrapped after early fan response. The released version returns to a "classic" green design fans had been asking for, harkening back to the kits worn by Hugo Sanchez (1986), Cuauhtemoc Blanco (1998 era), and Jared Borgetti. [houseofheat.co Mexico 2026 kit article]
- **Release status:** Official 2026 home kit on sale now (per adidas.com product page JL8537). Special "Adidas x Someone Somewhere" collab released May 11. [Instagram chuyfckits 2026-04-10; adidas.com]

### FMF and federation history

- **Founded:** August 23, 1927. Replaced the Federación Central de Fútbol. First president: Humberto Garza Ramos. [Wikipedia Mexican Football Federation; fmf.mx official; Grokipedia]
- **Predecessor history:** The Federación Mexicana de Football Asociación was formed in 1922 (under president Ulises Garza Ramos) by unifying two leagues; renamed Federación Central de Fútbol shortly after; replaced by current FMF in 1927. [Wikipedia]

### Kit history

- **Brand sequence:** Umbro -> Atletica -> Nike -> Adidas (current; Adidas returned for 2022 Qatar campaign and continues through 2026). [as.com Mexico kit evolution article; cultkits.com 2026-vs-1998 comparison; YouTube Sports Guru London kit evolution]
- **Iconic kits:** 1986 (Hugo Sanchez Mt. Rushmore reference), 1994, 1998 (cult-status, often top-of-list in all-time kit rankings), 2014, 2018, 2022 (Qatar away). [cultkits.com; as.com]
- **Color discipline:** Verde as primary on the home kit since 1968. Away kit alternates between white (most eras) and red (notably 1998). Third kit historically goes deep red or black. [Wikimedia Commons Mexico kits page]

### LA / diaspora / Rose Bowl

- **May 30, 2026 send-off match:** Mexico vs Australia at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena. Final pre-WC match in the US. Multi-day "send-off celebration" in the Los Angeles area. Tickets via SomosLocales.com starting 10:00 a.m. PT. [visitpasadena.com event listing; ticketmaster.com event page; rosebowlstadium.com news article 2026-04]
- **Pasadena Rose Bowl history:** Decades of Mexico friendlies. ABC7 covered "thousands of fans" at past Rose Bowl Mexico events for World Cup sendoffs. [abc7.com Rose Bowl WC sendoff article]
- **Strategic context:** Australia is hosted as a friendly opponent because per a-league-fans Reddit thread, "all games played in US are friendlies; only competitive games are played in Mexico." Confirms the Rose Bowl-as-friendly-venue pattern. [reddit.com r/Aleague]

### Legends (kit-history H2 anchor)

- **Hugo Sanchez:** Career 1977-1994. Mexican forward, prolific goalscorer, Real Madrid era legend. The 1986 Mexico kit at Azteca is the diaspora's emotional reference point for the Sanchez era. [Wikipedia Hugo Sanchez; Instagram BetAnything Sports top-5 Mexicans of all time]
- **Rafael Marquez:** "El Kaiser." Best defender in Mexican history, five World Cups (only the third player ever to feature in five). Currently assistant coach. [Wikipedia Rafael Marquez; espn.co.uk article]
