## EVERGREEN FRAMING NOTES (architectural review per session instructions)

This is the first canonical evergreen Tier 2B brief produced under codified discipline. Sibling evergreen Tier 2B candidates `/collections/adidas-soccer-cleats` (already produced in this batch) and `/collections/apparel` (pending). What worked, what surfaced, and what should template for future evergreen collection work:

### What worked

1. **Head-term primary keyword over slug-literal.** The slug `all-footwear` literally matches `soccer footwear` (110/mo, low). Choosing `soccer cleats` (201,000/mo) as primary aligns the page with where the volume actually lives. This is the inversion of the year-specificity rule that applies at PDP scope; at collection-page hero scope, the head term beats the slug-literal every time. Template-ready rule: for evergreen multi-brand collections, prioritize the highest-volume transactional head term that the page legitimately serves, not the slug-literal token.

2. **How-to-choose architecture for H2 framework.** Five evergreen-friendly H2s (Silos, Plate, Position, Tier Ladder, How to Pick) plus one contained catalyst (Current Drops). This framework survives every brand refresh and every season turnover. The catalyst H2 is the only one that needs updating when brands ship the next pack. Template-ready: silo + plate + position + tier + catalyst framework should be the default H2 architecture for any cross-brand category collection.

3. **Brand-by-silo within H2 1, NOT brand-by-brand as separate H2s.** Initial draft considered "adidas H2 / Nike H2 / Puma H2" architecture. Rejected because: (a) brand-specific H2s belong on brand-specific collection pages, not the top-of-funnel umbrella; (b) the buyer at this page is making a brand-cross-shopping decision, not committing to one brand yet; (c) brand-by-brand H2 structure would push the page over 1,000 words. Template-ready: at top-of-funnel umbrella collections, integrate brands within how-to-choose H2 structure rather than giving each brand its own H2.

4. **Cross-avatar surface for Jennifer + Carlos within Tyler-primary architecture.** The Tier Ladder + Kids Cut H2 explicitly serves Jennifer (parent buying for child) without breaking the Tyler-anchored voice. Current Drops H2 explicitly serves Carlos (boot-drop chaser) by naming signature packs and player editions. This multi-avatar service is possible because the page is genuinely top-of-funnel : all four avatars legitimately land here at different intent surfaces. Template-ready: at hero category pages, name all four avatar landing surfaces in the workforce briefing and integrate them into the H2 structure rather than forcing single-avatar focus.

5. **Cognitive Load Minimization rule on jargon stacking.** The Plate Selection H2 defines FG, AG, SG, MG, TF, IC inline once per acronym ("Firm Ground (FG) is for natural grass on a maintained pitch") before using the acronyms later. This is the explicit Cognitive Load Minimization rule from `context/03-brand-voice.md` and it pays off on a top-of-funnel page where some readers (Jennifer) need the definitions and some (Tyler) don't but won't be condescended to either. Template-ready: jargon stacking on top-of-funnel pages always requires the playbook's define-once-then-use pattern.

6. **Internal link selection to brand-specific child collections over surface-specific.** Adidas + Nike collection links provide a clear browse-funnel architecture: hero (this page) → brand (adidas-soccer-cleats / nike-soccer-shoes-cleats) → silo (adidas-predator / nike-mercurial) → product. Surface-specific collections (firm-ground-cleats, indoor-soccer-shoes) would short-circuit this hierarchy. Template-ready: at top-of-funnel hero pages, prefer broader-catalog-destination links to brand-specific child collections over surface-specific or feature-specific collections.

### Architectural questions surfaced

1. **Body word count for hero category pages.** The playbook target is 200-500 words. This brief lands at 685 words. The topic breadth (3 big brand silos + specialty brands + 5 surface plates + 4 tier ladders + kids cut + current drops) genuinely warrants the additional depth. Mexico v5 (canonical Tier 2B) sits at similar length and was approved. **Recommendation for playbook refinement:** consider a tiered word-count target: 200-400 for narrow category collections (single-brand, single-surface, single-avatar), 400-700 for hero category collections (multi-brand, multi-surface, multi-avatar) when topical depth warrants. Hard ceiling at 700 to prevent mobile burial.

2. **Slug preservation vs head-term-match slug.** The slug `all-footwear` is suboptimal for the head term `soccer cleats`. A renamed slug like `soccer-cleats` would align with the primary keyword but would break inbound PDP links across 6,000+ footwear products in the catalog (every PDP that links back to the hero footwear collection would 404 or need redirect). Preserving the existing slug is the right call per the playbook's existing-slug preservation rule. **Architectural question:** is there a future cleanup project where slug-rename + redirect-map work is justified for the highest-traffic hero collections? Surface as VERITAS follow-up if Mike wants to consider it later.

3. **FAQ inclusion threshold.** The 3-criteria test (real buyer questions with volume + not addressed in body + net-new value) failed for this brief because the body H2s on plate and position selection already cover the major buyer questions surfaced in the PAA module ("What cleats are better for soccer?" "What cleats do soccer pros wear?"). FAQ defaulted to SKIP. **Recommendation:** the FAQ SKIP default is correct here, but worth flagging that hero category pages with rich PAA modules MIGHT earn FAQ if the body H2 architecture doesn't naturally cover the buyer questions. This brief's how-to-choose H2 architecture absorbed the PAA topics; a brand-by-brand H2 architecture would have left them open and might have earned FAQ.

4. **Currency check decay.** Brand silo lineups change with each major release cycle (typically 12-18 months per silo). The Current Drops H2 references the 2026 spring lineup specifically. Template-ready maintenance check: any hero category brief should carry a workforce-internal note flagging the catalyst H2 as the maintenance surface : when the brands ship the next major pack (estimated H2 2026 for Mercurial Vapor 17 launch, late 2026 for Adidas Born For Goals II pack), the Current Drops H2 needs updating but the rest of the brief stays.

### Recommended template refinement candidates for evergreen hero category collections

1. **Codify a tiered word-count target** in the collection-page playbook: 200-400 narrow category, 400-700 hero category. Mexico v5 + this brief support the higher range for hero pages.

2. **Codify head-term-over-slug-literal rule** for hero category collections in the keyword distribution section: "When the slug matches a low-volume token and a higher-volume head term legitimately covers the page scope, the head term is primary; the slug stays preserved."

3. **Codify the how-to-choose H2 architecture** for cross-brand category collections: silo + plate + position + tier + catalyst as the default framework. Brand-by-brand H2 structure is for brand-specific collections, not the top-of-funnel umbrella.

4. **Codify the broader-catalog-destination preference** specifically for hero category collection internal links: prefer brand-specific child collections over surface-specific collections at this tier.

5. **Consider adding an "Architecture: top-of-funnel umbrella collection" worked example** to the collection-page playbook alongside the existing national-team, player, and brand-category worked examples. This brief could serve as that worked example once the four refinements above are codified.
