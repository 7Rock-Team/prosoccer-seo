# For Jorge: 25 product pages Google is sending customers to, with nothing to buy

**Prepared:** 2026-08-27  |  **From:** Mike  |  **Stock checked live:** 2026-08-27

## What this is

Out of every product page on the site, these are 25 of the 26 that Google shows most often.
Over the last 90 days they were shown in search results **3,051,280 times** between them.

**Every one of them is completely out of stock.** Not low, not partly. Across all 25 pages there
are 129 size and colour options in total, and **zero of the 129 can be added to a cart.**

Out of three million times shown, only 8,439 people clicked. That is about 1 click for every
362 times the page appeared. For pages sitting this high in the results, that is far below what
they would normally get, and the most likely reason is that people who do land there find
nothing available and leave.

## The question

For each of these, one of two things is true, and only you can say which:

1. **It is coming back.** Then the question is when, and whether the reorder is already placed.
2. **It is not coming back.** Then the question is whether there is a current-season equivalent
   that should take its place, since the demand is clearly still there.

Roughly half of these are **current 2026 season** items, which is the more surprising half.
These are not old leftovers. They are this year's product, added to the site between November
2025 and May 2026, already sold out in every size, during a World Cup year.

The other half are older, from the 2019 through 2025 seasons. For those the realistic question
is probably the replacement one rather than the restock one.

## One small separate thing, not urgent

Item 14 on the list, the Predator Freak .3 Laceless, has the brand name capitalised in its
stored Shopify product name. The brand writes its own name in lower case, adidas, everywhere
including on their own site, so that product record is capitalised where it should not be.
Nothing breaks because of it and it does not affect stock. It is only worth a look next time
someone is in that record anyway.

## What we are not asking

Nothing here is a request to change the website, the product pages, or how they are written.
This is only about whether the stock is coming back and what should happen to the pages where
it is not. If the answer for a given item is "no restock, no replacement," that is a complete
answer and we will handle the page from our side.
