## Fit Notes
The Vapor runs low and snug, true to the Mercurial speed fit. That's a narrow, locked-down feel. If your foot runs wide, size up a half or try them on first. Full size run, M4/W5.5 to M13/W14.5.

## Care and Maintenance
Knock the mud off after every match. Wipe the upper with a damp cloth and air dry them away from heaters and direct sun. Never put them in the dryer, the heat warps the plate.

## FAQs about the Nike Vapor 17 Academy
### Are these any good on turf?
Yes. The chevron studs are rated for Firm Ground and Multi-Ground, so they cover natural grass and artificial grass. For older rubber-pellet turf, a dedicated turf shoe is the smarter pick.

### Do they have a collar like the Superfly?
No. The Vapor 17 Academy is the low-cut version. If you want the mid-height Dynamic Fit collar, that's the Superfly you're after.

### How do they fit?
Snug and narrow, like the rest of the Mercurial line. True to size for regular and narrow feet. Wide feet, size up or come try a pair in Pasadena.
