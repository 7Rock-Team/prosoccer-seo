### Long Description (body copy)

## El Tri at the 2026 World Cup

Mexico opens against South Africa on June 11 at Estadio Azteca. Same matchup that opened the 2010 tournament, sixteen years on. Group A goes through South Korea and Czechia, the latter coming through the European playoffs. Up to five matches on home soil if El Tri tops the group, with knockout rounds potentially staying at Azteca through the round of 16. The stadium becomes the first in the world to host three opening matches: 1970, 1986, and now 2026.

## The 2026 Home Kit

Adidas reached for the Aztec heritage on this one. The shirt carries pre-Hispanic motifs across the chest, climacool fabric for Mexican summer heat, and the FMF crest centered traditional. This is the second design Adidas tried; the first version, with the flag colors running down the center, got scrapped after early fan response leaned classic. The released kit sits closer to Hugo Sanchez's '86 and Cuauhtemoc Blanco's '98 than to anything Adidas has shipped recently. The diaspora has been asking for this exact kit back.

## Verde, Blanco, y Rojo: The Colors of El Tri

The colors are the flag. The eagle on the crest comes from the Mexican coat of arms, the same eagle on the peso. Verde for hope. Blanco for unity. Rojo for the blood of the nation. Every El Tri home kit since 1968 has held verde as the primary, the eagle centered, the away alternating between white (most eras) and red (notably '98). The 2026 third kit lands closer to the tournament; Adidas usually goes deep red or black for the third.

## Coach Aguirre and the Players to Watch

Javier Aguirre is back for his third World Cup as El Tri manager, with Rafa Marquez (the best defender in Mexican history, the only Mexican to play in five World Cups) as assistant. Edson Alvarez anchors the double pivot, the squad's metronome at West Ham. Hirving "Chucky" Lozano on the wing, the speed run that opens the box. Santiago Gimenez at striker after the move from Feyenoord to Milan, the goal-scorer the team's been searching for since Hugo. Raul Jimenez carries veteran weight; Alexis Vega and Henry Martin compete for the second striker spot. Aguirre names the final 26 in late May.

## Kit Heritage from 1986 to 2026

The 1986 kit, the home Mexico wore at Azteca for that World Cup, is the diaspora's reference point. The 1998 home (cult-status, top-of-list in most all-time kit rankings) ran a clean green with the FMF crest large across the chest. Atletica took over from Umbro for a stretch in the early 2000s. Nike held the contract through 2014 and 2018. Adidas returned for 2022's Qatar campaign and stays through 2026, with the contract running deeper into the next cycle.

## Why El Tri Means More in LA

LA County has more Mexicans than any city outside Mexico City. The Rose Bowl in Pasadena has hosted El Tri friendlies for decades; the May 30 send-off match against Australia is the team's last stop in the US before kickoff. When Mexico plays at the Rose Bowl, the green stands aren't an away crowd. They're the home crowd. The 2026 kit isn't fan merch; it's the flag a city wears in shifts.

## Mexico Jersey FAQs

**When does the 2026 home kit release?**
The official Adidas 2026 home kit is on sale now. A special Adidas x Someone Somewhere collab released May 11. The away kit follows ahead of the tournament; the third typically lands closer to the opener.

**What's the difference between the player and fan version of the El Tri kit?**
Player version uses Adidas Heat.RDY weave with a closer cut and heat-bonded badges, the kit the squad actually wears on the pitch. Fan version uses softer climacool fabric with a regular fit and stitched badges. Same look from across the room. Different feel up close. Player version typically runs $30 to $50 more.

**Who's likely on Mexico's 2026 World Cup squad?**
Coach Aguirre named twenty Liga MX players to the pre-tournament camp in May; twelve are guaranteed final-26 spots. Fourteen Europe-based slots round out the squad. Confirmed names include Edson Alvarez, Hirving Lozano, Santiago Gimenez, Raul Jimenez, Alexis Vega, and Henry Martin. The final 26-man list lands in late May.

**Why does Mexico wear green?**
The flag. Verde for hope, blanco for unity, rojo for the blood of the nation. The eagle on the crest is from the Mexican coat of arms. Every El Tri home kit since 1968 has worn green as the primary; the away has alternated between white and red across eras.

**Where can I get the kit with Chucky Lozano's name and number?**
Both player and fan versions can be customized. Standard names like Lozano (#22), Gimenez, Alvarez, and Jimenez ship from stock; custom names take five to seven business days for personalization.

**What size should I get for a teen between sizes?**
Adidas youth sizes run a size smaller than US-standard youth (Adidas YL is closer to a US small). For kids between sizes, size up; the kit stretches more at the chest and shoulder than at the waist hem.

**Has Estadio Azteca really hosted three World Cup opening matches?**
Yes: 1970, 1986, and 2026. No other stadium in the world has hosted three. Mexico becomes the first country to host three World Cups when the tournament kicks off (co-host with the US and Canada in 2026 makes it official).
