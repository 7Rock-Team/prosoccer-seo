## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does the F50 Elite Laceless run true to size?
It runs true to size for most players, but fit is more important on a laceless cleat because there are no laces to tighten. The Haloskin+ upper fits close and snug. If you have a narrow foot or like a locked-in feel, the standard size is right. If your foot is wider or higher in volume, go up a half size, or consider the laced F50 Elite so you can dial the fit in with the laces.

### What's the difference between the laceless F50 and the regular laced F50 Elite?
Same speed chassis, same Haloskin+ upper, same firm-ground plate. The laceless version removes the laces for one clean strike surface across the forefoot and a quicker step-in. The laced version gives you adjustable lockdown, which suits wider feet or anyone who likes to crank the fit tight. If you want the purest, most even contact and your foot matches the shape, go laceless. If you want fine control over fit, go laced.

### Should I get the Elite, Pro, or League version?
Elite is the top tier, the full laceless speed build with the Haloskin+ upper the pros wear. Pro keeps most of the performance with slightly heavier materials to lower the price. League is the club-and-academy option, built more for durability than for shaving grams. If the pure laceless feel is the whole point, Elite is the one.

### Can I wear these on artificial turf?
No, these are firm-ground (FG) cleats made for dry natural grass. The stud pattern grips real grass and can feel harsh on artificial surfaces. If you play mostly on turf or artificial grass, look for the turf or AG version in the F50 line.
