## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does the F50 Hyperfast League Mid run true to size?
It runs true to size for most players. The upper fits close and the mid-cut collar adds a snug feel over the ankle, so if your foot is narrow or you like a tight fit, the standard size works well. Wider feet may want a half size up for a touch more room.

### How is the mid-cut collar different from a low-cut F50?
The mid-cut collar sits above the ankle and wraps it with knit for extra lockdown and support during sprints and sharp cuts. A low-cut F50 stops below the ankle and saves a few grams for a barely-there feel. Pick the Mid if you want the ankle held steady; pick the low-cut if you want the lightest possible setup.

### Are these cleats good for artificial turf?
No, these are firm-ground (FG) cleats made for dry natural grass. The stud pattern grips real grass and can feel harsh on artificial surfaces. If you play mostly on turf, look at the F50 League turf version instead.

### What's the difference between the League, Pro, and Elite F50?
Elite is the top tier with the lightest premium build the pros wear. Pro keeps most of that performance at a friendlier price. League, this cleat, is the accessible tier built to last a club or academy season while keeping the F50 speed feel. If you want F50 pace without the top-tier price, League is the one.
