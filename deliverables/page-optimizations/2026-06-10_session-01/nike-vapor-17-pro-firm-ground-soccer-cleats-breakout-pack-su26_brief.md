## Care and Maintenance
- Air-dry after every match; stuff with newspaper to hold the shape
- Keep them away from heat, sun, and the dryer, which break down the Flyknit and warp the plate

- Store at room temperature, not a hot trunk or a damp gym bag

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does the Vapor 17 Pro run true to size?
It runs true to size for most players. The Vapor is a narrow-to-standard cleat, and the Pro's full Flyknit upper sits a little more forgiving than the Elite's AtomKnit. If your foot is narrow you'll lock in at your standard size; if it's wider, go up a half size for a bit more room.

### What's the difference between the Vapor 17 Pro and the Elite?
Both are the low-cut Vapor built for quickness in tight spaces. The Elite uses Nike's lightest AtomKnit upper and FlyLite plate for the most stripped-back, near-barefoot feel, and it's the priciest tier. The Pro keeps the same Vapor agility identity with a full Flyknit upper, a sock-like wrap, and a slightly more forgiving fit, for a good bit less money. If you want the Vapor game without the Elite spend, the Pro is the one.

### Should I get the Vapor or the Superfly?
Pick the Vapor if your game is quick feet in close: the touch out of pressure, the chop inside, the sharp cut before a shot. The Superfly leans toward straight-line acceleration and repeat sprints down the line. Both are Mercurials; the Vapor is the Mad Quick one. You can see the full lineup on the [Nike Mercurial collection](https://www.prosoccer.com/collections/nike-mercurial).

### Are these cleats good for turf or artificial grass?
No, these are firm-ground (FG) cleats made for dry natural grass. The chevron stud pattern is built to bite real grass and can feel harsh on turf or artificial surfaces. If you play mostly on those, look for the turf or AG version instead. The rest of this drop lives on the [Breakout Pack collection](https://www.prosoccer.com/collections/nike-breakout-soccer-cleats).
