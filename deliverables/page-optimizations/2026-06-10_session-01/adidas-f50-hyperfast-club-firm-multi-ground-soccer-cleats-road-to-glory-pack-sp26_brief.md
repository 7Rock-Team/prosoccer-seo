## Care and Maintenance
- Air-dry after every match; stuff with newspaper to pull moisture and hold the shape
- Keep them away from heat, sun, and the dryer, which break down the upper and warp the sole
- Brush off dried grass and turf pellets before storing; the synthetic upper needs no conditioner
- Store at room temperature, not a hot trunk or a damp gym bag

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Can I wear the F50 Club on both grass and turf?
Yes, that's the point of this one. The firm-multi-ground (FG/MG) sole is built to grip dry natural grass and still feel right on artificial turf, so it's a solid pick if you split your week between the two surfaces. For dedicated soft, wet fields or a strict turf-only setup, a surface-specific cleat would serve you better.

### Is the Club version a big step down from the F50 Elite?
It's a different tier for a different need. Elite is the $270 top build with the lightest materials the pros wear. The Club keeps the F50 speed feel and the FG/MG versatility at $64.99, trading some of the featherweight construction for a price a developing or budget-minded player can hit. If you want F50 pace without the top-tier spend, the Club is the one.

### Does the F50 Club run true to size?
It runs true to size for most players. The synthetic upper fits close but doesn't pinch, so your normal number usually works. If your foot is on the wider side, going up a half size gives you a little more room.

### Should I pick the F50 over the Predator or Copa?
Go F50 if your game is speed and getting in behind defenders. The Predator leans toward control and a heavier strike, and the Copa toward a soft, cushioned touch. The F50 is the speed line.
