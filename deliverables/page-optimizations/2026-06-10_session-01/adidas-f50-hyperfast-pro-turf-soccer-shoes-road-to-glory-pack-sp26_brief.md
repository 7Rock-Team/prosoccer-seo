## Care and Maintenance
- Air-dry naturally after play; stuff with newspaper to hold the shape
- Keep them away from heat and dryers (heat warps the outsole and breaks down the upper)
- Brush off dried turf pellets with a soft brush; the synthetic Haloskin+ upper needs no conditioner, just a damp-cloth wipe
- Store at room temperature

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do the F50 Hyperfast Pro Turf run true to size?
For most players, yes. The fit is regular through the midfoot but sits snug like a speed shoe should. If you're between sizes or want a little more room in the toe, size up a half.

### What's the difference between the Pro and the Elite F50?
The Pro carries the same Haloskin+ upper feel as the Elite on a more conventional, everyday plate. You keep the touch and lockdown that make the F50 fast, and you pay quite a bit less. The Elite is the top-tier, most responsive build; the Pro is the value pick for nearly everyone.

### Can I wear these on natural grass or firm ground?
These are turf (TF) shoes, built for synthetic grass with a rubber outsole. For natural grass you'll want a firm-ground (FG) plate instead. If your home pitch is real grass, look at the F50 Pro FG version rather than this one.

### What surfaces are turf shoes actually best for?
Older astroturf, all-weather synthetic pitches, and the turf fields that stay playable when grass ones are waterlogged. The rubber outsole spreads pressure across the surface and grips without the long studs that don't belong on synthetic grass.
