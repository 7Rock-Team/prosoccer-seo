## Care and Maintenance
- Air-dry after every session; stuff with newspaper to pull moisture and hold the shape
- Keep them out of heat, sun, and dryers, which break down the upper and the rubber outsole
- Brush off turf pellets before storing; the synthetic upper needs only a soft brush, no conditioner
- Store at room temperature, not a hot trunk or a damp gym bag

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What surface are the F50 League Turf shoes made for?
They're built for artificial and synthetic turf, including older astroturf and modern 3G pitches. The flat rubber outsole spreads grip across the surface so you don't slide on hard ground. For natural grass you'd want a firm-ground (FG) version of the F50 instead.

### Do the F50 League Turf shoes run true to size?
Yes, true to size for most players. The Haloskin upper fits close and snug, so a narrow foot locks in well at the standard size. If your foot runs wide, go up a half size for a little more room.

### What's the difference between the League turf shoe and the Pro or Elite F50?
League is the accessible tier, the F50 speed game at a price built for regular play. Pro and Elite move up to lighter, more performance-focused materials at a higher price. If you're on turf most weeks and want the F50 feel without the top-tier cost, the League turf shoe is the smart pick. You can compare the full lineup on the [adidas F50 collection](https://www.prosoccer.com/collections/adidas-f50).

### Can I wear turf shoes on natural grass?
You can, but it's not ideal. The flat turf outsole doesn't dig into soft grass the way studs do, so you lose some grip when the ground gets wet or loose. If you split time between surfaces, look at a firm-multi ground option.
