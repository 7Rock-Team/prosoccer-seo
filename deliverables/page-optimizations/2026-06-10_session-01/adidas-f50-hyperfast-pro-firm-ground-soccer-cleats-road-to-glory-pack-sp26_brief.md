## Care and Maintenance
- Air-dry after every match; stuff with newspaper to hold the shape
- Keep them off heat, direct sun, and dryers, which break down the upper and warp the plate
- Brush off dried grass with a soft brush; the synthetic Haloskin+ upper needs no conditioner
- Store at room temperature, not a hot trunk or a damp gym bag

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is the F50 Pro worth it over the Elite?
For most players, yes. The Pro keeps the F50 speed build, the lightweight chassis, the firm-ground plate, and the close-fitting Haloskin+ upper, and trims cost with slightly less premium materials than the Elite. If you're playing every week and want top-end pace without the top-end price, the Pro is the smart pick. The Elite is for players chasing every last gram.

### Does the F50 Hyperfast Pro run true to size?
It runs true to size for most players. The upper fits close and snug right out of the box, so a narrow foot or a player who likes a tight feel can stay at the standard size. Wider feet are usually more comfortable a half size up.

### Can I wear these on artificial turf?
No, these are firm-ground (FG) cleats made for dry natural grass. The stud pattern is built to grip real grass and can feel harsh and wear faster on artificial surfaces. If you mostly play on turf or artificial grass, look at the F50 Pro Turf or an AG option instead.

### Should I pick the F50, the Predator, or the Copa?
Go with the F50 if speed is your game: wingers, fast strikers, and full-backs who beat defenders with acceleration. The Predator leans toward control, power, and spin, and the Copa toward a soft, natural touch. The F50 is the speed silo.
