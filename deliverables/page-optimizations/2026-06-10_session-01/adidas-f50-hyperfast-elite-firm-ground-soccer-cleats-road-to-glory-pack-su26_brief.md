## Care and Maintenance
- Air-dry after each match; stuff with newspaper to hold the shape
- Keep them out of heat, sun, and dryers, which break down the upper and warp the plate
- Brush off dried grass; the synthetic Haloskin+ upper needs only a soft brush, no conditioner
- Store at room temperature, not a hot trunk or damp gym bag

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does the F50 Hyperfast Elite run true to size?
It runs true to size for most players. The Haloskin+ upper fits close and snug right away, so if you have a narrow foot or like a tight feel, the standard size works well. If your foot is wider, going up a half size gives you a little more room.

### What's the difference between the F50 Elite and the Pro or League versions?
Elite is the top tier, the full speed build with the Haloskin+ upper and Halocage+ skin the pros actually wear. Pro keeps most of that performance with slightly heavier materials to bring the price down. League is the accessible club-and-academy option built more for durability than for shaving grams. If pure top-end speed is the point, Elite is the one.

### Are these cleats good for artificial turf?
No, these are firm-ground (FG) cleats made for dry natural grass. The stud pattern is built to grip real grass and can feel harsh on artificial surfaces. If you mostly play on turf, look for the AG or turf version in the F50 line instead.

### Who should wear the F50 instead of the Predator or Copa?
Pick the F50 if your game is speed: wingers, strikers, and fast full-backs who beat defenders with acceleration. The Predator leans toward control and power, and the Copa toward touch and a softer feel. The F50 is the speed cleat in the adidas lineup.
