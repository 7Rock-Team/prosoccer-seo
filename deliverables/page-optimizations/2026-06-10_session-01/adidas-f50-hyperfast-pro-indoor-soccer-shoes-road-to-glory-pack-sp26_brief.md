The F50 has always been adidas' speed shoe, and the Hyperfast Pro brings that idea indoors. Outdoors, F50 speed is the long run in behind. Indoors, it's pure reaction. ## Fit Notes

The F50 Hyperfast runs close and snug, the way a speed shoe should, and fits true to size for most players. Wider feet may want a half size up, though the Pro is more forgiving than the laceless top-tier models. No studs to break in, so they're ready out of the box. If you're between sizes, the snug speed-shoe build means most players are better off at their normal size than going up.

## Care and Maintenance

- Wipe the outsole down after play so dust doesn't dull the grip
- Brush off court scuffs with a soft, dry brush before they set
- Air-dry away from heaters and sun; heat warps the sole and stiffens the upper
- Stuff with newspaper after sweaty sessions to pull moisture and hold shape

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What surfaces are these adidas F50 indoor shoes made for?

Flat indoor surfaces: gym hardwood, sport-court flooring, and futsal courts. The non-marking rubber outsole grips on those without leaving scuff marks. They are not built for grass or turf, so if you play on those too, you'll want a separate FG or turf pair.

### How is the Pro different from the F50 Elite indoor shoe?

The Pro carries the same Haloskin+ upper as the Elite, so the feel on the ball is nearly identical. The difference is the platform and lockdown system underneath, which keeps the Pro lighter on the wallet while holding most of the on-ball performance. For indoor and futsal, it's the value pick of the F50 range.

### Do the F50 Hyperfast Pro indoor shoes run true to size?

Yes, true to size for most players, with a snug speed-shoe fit. Wider feet may prefer going half a size up. There are no studs to break in, so they're comfortable from the first session.

### Are these good for futsal as well as indoor soccer?

They are. The flat non-marking sole and quick, reactive feel suit the tight, fast futsal game just as well as indoor five-a-side. Quick touches and sharp cuts in small space are exactly what this shoe is tuned for.
