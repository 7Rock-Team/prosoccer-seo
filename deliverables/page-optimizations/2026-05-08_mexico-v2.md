## Mexico Jersey FAQs

Six answers to the questions you're already asking yourself.

**What's the difference between the player version and the fan version?**
Player version uses lighter, sweat-wicking fabric and a closer cut to the body. Fan version uses softer fabric and a regular fit. Player version typically runs $30 to $50 more.

**Are these jerseys authentic Adidas, or replicas?**
Every Mexico jersey on this page is sourced direct from Adidas, with the official tags, holographic, and Federation badge. We don't carry replicas pretending to be authentic.

**When does the 2026 World Cup home kit drop?**
The home kit typically drops six to eight weeks before the tournament. The away follows about four weeks before kickoff. Sign up for the drop list and we'll text you when your size lands.

**What sizes are available for kids?**
Youth XS through XL, plus adult cuts. Note that Adidas youth large fits closer to an adult small. If your kid is between sizes, the Pasadena fitting room is the fastest way to confirm before you commit.

**Can I get a Mexico jersey with a name and number?**
Yes. Customization is available on both the fan version and the player version. Standard names (Chicharito, Lozano, Raul) ship from stock; custom names take five to seven business days.

**What's your return policy on Mexico jerseys?**
30 days, original tags attached. If the size is wrong, exchange is free. Custom name-and-number jerseys are final sale once printing starts.
