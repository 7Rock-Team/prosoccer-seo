## Care and Maintenance

- Air-dry after every match; stuff with newspaper to hold shape and pull moisture
- Keep them away from direct heat, sunlight, and the dryer, which breaks down the upper and warps the plate
- Brush off dried grass and dirt with a soft brush before storing
- The synthetic VNMSkin and Flyknit upper needs no conditioner, just a soft brush and a wipe with a damp cloth
- Store at room temperature, not in a hot car or a cold garage

## FAQs about the Jr Phantom 6 Low Pro

### Does the Jr Phantom 6 Low Pro run true to size?

It runs true to size for most young players, with a slightly roomier toe box than Nike's speed cleats. For a wider foot or a growth spurt on the way, the natural-fit frame leaves a little room without feeling loose.

### What surfaces can my kid play on in these?

The firm/multi-ground (FMG) plate is the versatile one. It grips firm natural grass for club matches and holds up on the harder, mixed surfaces at school and the park. It is not built for dedicated artificial-turf carpets or indoor courts; those need AG or IC cleats.

### What is the difference between the Low Pro and the Academy junior versions?

The Low Pro sits in the Pro tier with the VNMSkin control upper and the molded shoe frame, a real step up in touch and feel. The Academy versions are the more entry-level junior tier at a lower price. If your player is getting serious about control, the Low Pro is the upgrade.

### Is this a good first serious cleat for a developing player?

Yes. It brings Phantom control to junior feet at a Pro-tier level without the flagship price, and the FMG plate covers most of the surfaces a growing player runs into week to week.
