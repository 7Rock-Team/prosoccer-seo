## The taegeuk, and the red behind it
Korea play as the Taegeuk Warriors, named for the red-and-blue taegeuk at the center of the national flag, the old symbol of balance and harmony. Red has been the team's home color for decades, the reason fans simply call them the Reds. The heritage runs deep: back-to-back continental titles in 1956 and 1960, and a semi-final run on home soil in 2002 that beat Italy and Spain along the way and made Korea the first team from outside Europe and the Americas to reach that stage. The Korea Football Association runs the team, which competes under the AFC, and Nike has outfitted the Reds for years. Wearing this shirt puts you in that line.
