## Fit Notes

The Phantom 6 runs true to size for most players. If you have a narrow foot or want the closest possible touch, try going down a half size. The high collar takes a session or two to settle, so break it in at training before a match day.

## Care and Maintenance

- Air-dry naturally after every match; stuff with newspaper to hold the shape and pull moisture
- Keep them away from direct heat, sunlight, and dryers, which break down the Gripknit upper and can warp the plate
- Brush off dried grass and dirt with a soft brush before storing
- Synthetic upper: a soft-brush clean is enough, no leather conditioner needed
- Store at room temperature, out of a damp bag

## FAQs about the Phantom 6 High Elite FG

### Does the Phantom 6 High Elite FG run true to size?

It runs true to size for most players. If you prefer a snug, close-to-the-ball fit or have a narrow foot, go down a half size. The upper does not stretch much, so avoid sizing up.

### Can I wear these on artificial grass?

The FG plate is built for dry, natural grass and well-kept firm pitches. If you play most of your soccer on artificial turf, the AG version of the Phantom 6 Shadow is the better plate for that surface.

### What is different about the Shadow Pack colorway?

The Shadow Pack is the FA26 release in a darker, green-tinged finish (shown here in Black/Black/Illusion Green). The cleat's build is the standard High Elite Phantom 6; the Shadow Pack is the seasonal colorway treatment across several Nike models.

### Why the high collar instead of the low-cut version?

The Dynamic Fit collar wraps the ankle for a locked-in, secure feel on hard plants and turns. If you want a lighter, freer feel at the ankle for faster release, the Low Elite Phantom 6 gives you the same Elite control without the collar.
