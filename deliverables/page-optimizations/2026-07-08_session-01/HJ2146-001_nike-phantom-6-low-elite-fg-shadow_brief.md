## Fit Notes

The new frame opens the toe box a touch and molds to the foot as you wear it in, so a wide foot has more room here than earlier Phantoms gave. If you like a locked-in, snug fit, going down a half size is safe.

## Care and Maintenance

- Air-dry after every match; stuff with newspaper to hold shape and pull moisture
- Keep them away from direct heat, sunlight, and dryers, which break down the upper and warp the plate
- Brush off dried grass and dirt with a soft brush before storing
- The Gripknit upper is synthetic, so a soft brush is usually all it needs; no conditioner required
- Store at room temperature, out of a hot car or a damp bag

## FAQs about the Phantom 6 Low Elite FG

### Does the Phantom 6 Low Elite FG run true to size?

It runs true to size for most players. The new frame gives the toe box a little more room than older Phantoms, so wider feet fit comfortably. If you want a snug, locked-in feel or have a narrow foot, drop a half size.

### What surface are these built for?

The FG (firm-ground) plate is made for dry, natural-grass fields. It is not the right pick for artificial turf, where the AG version of the Phantom 6 spreads pressure better and is easier on your legs.

### What colorway is this, and is it part of a pack?

This is the Shadow Pack (FA26) colorway, Black with Illusion Green. It sits in Nike's Shadow Pack, a run of dark-based cleats across the Phantom and Vapor lines that share the same stealth look.

### How is the Low different from the High Elite version?

Both are the same Elite-tier Phantom 6 with the Gripknit upper. The Low has no collar over the ankle, which frees the plant-and-go for players who want the quickest release; the High adds a wrapped collar for players who prefer the locked-in feel around the ankle.
