## Fit Notes

This cleat runs true to size for most players. The Flyknit upper hugs a low-to-medium-volume foot best; wider feet should stay at their normal size and let the knit give rather than sizing up. For a locked-in feel, some players go a snug half-size down.

## Care and Maintenance

- Air-dry after every match; stuff with newspaper to hold shape and pull moisture
- Keep away from direct heat, sunlight, and dryers, which warp the plate and stiffen the upper
- Brush the Flyknit upper clean with a soft brush before storing; no leather conditioner needed

## FAQs about the Vapor 17 Pro FG

### Does the Nike Vapor 17 Pro run true to size?

It runs true to size for most players. The Flyknit upper wraps a low-to-medium-volume foot closely, so wider feet should stay at their normal size and let the knit ease in. Players who want a locked-in feel for tight-space play sometimes go a snug half-size down.

### What surface are these cleats made for?

The firm-ground plate and chevron studs are built for dry, natural-grass fields. For artificial grass, a dedicated AG plate is the safer long-term choice, since FG studs wear faster and grip differently on synthetic turf.

### What is the Shadow Pack colorway?

This Vapor 17 Pro is the Shadow Pack (FA26) release, a blacked-out Flyknit upper with Illusion Green accents at the sole. It reads dark on the foot and flashes green when you move.

### How is the Vapor 17 Pro different from the Vapor 17 Elite?

Both share the low-cut Vapor shape and agility focus. The Pro sits a step below the Elite's stripped-back package, so it comes in a little heavier and at a lower price while keeping the close touch and quick-release feel the Vapor line is known for.
