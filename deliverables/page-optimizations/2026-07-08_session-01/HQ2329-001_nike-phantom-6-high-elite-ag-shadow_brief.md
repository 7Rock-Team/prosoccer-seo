## Care and Maintenance

- Air-dry naturally after every match; stuff with newspaper to hold shape and pull moisture
- Keep them out of direct heat, sunlight, and dryers, which break down the synthetic upper and warp the plate
- Brush dried turf pellets and dirt off the studs and Gripknit upper with a soft brush before storing
- Wipe the collar and upper with a damp cloth; the synthetic Gripknit needs no leather conditioner
- Store at room temperature, away from a hot trunk or a cold garage

## FAQs about the Phantom 6 High Elite AG

### Does the Phantom 6 High Elite AG run true to size?

It runs true to size for most players. The new frame molds to the foot through the toe box, so a narrow foot sits snug at your normal size and a wider foot gets natural room without a long break-in. If you are between sizes and want it locked, go down a half size.

### What is the difference between the AG and FG versions?

The AG (artificial grass) plate carries shorter conical studs made for longer synthetic surfaces, spreading the plant across the turf for a clean release. The FG (firm ground) version is built for natural grass and well-kept pitches. If most of your football is on 3G or synthetic, the AG is the plate matched to your surface.

### What colorway is the Shadow Pack version?

This one is Black/Black/Illusion Green: a blacked-out upper with a green flash, part of Nike's FA26 Shadow Pack. It is the low-profile look for a player who likes to go unnoticed until the cut is already made.

### Should I get the high collar or the low version?

The high collar adds a Dynamic Fit Flyknit wrap around the ankle for a secure, locked feel, which suits players who want that snug connection through fast turns. The low version frees the ankle for a lighter, more open feel. Both share the same Gripknit upper and AG-Pro plate, so the choice is collar preference, not performance tier.
