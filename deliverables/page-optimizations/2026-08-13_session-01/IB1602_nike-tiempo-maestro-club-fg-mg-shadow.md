Tiempo leans toward comfort rather than a race-tight fit, so most players get along with these out of the box. Order your player's normal size, and go up a half with thick match socks.

## Care and Maintenance

- Brush off dried grass and dirt after matches
- Air-dry at room temperature, stuffed with newspaper
- Keep them out of the dryer and off radiators; heat warps the plate
- Skip leather conditioner, the synthetic upper doesn't need it
- Wipe with a damp cloth, no harsh cleaners

Club is the entry tier in the Tiempo Maestro line. It keeps the touch-first upper and the multi-surface plate with simpler materials.
