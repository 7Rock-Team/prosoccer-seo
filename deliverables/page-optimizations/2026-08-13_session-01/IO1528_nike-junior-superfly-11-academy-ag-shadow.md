Artificial grass doesn't give the way real turf does. The studs on this plate are hollowed out, so load spreads across the sole instead of driving into one point. Your kid plants and turns without the surface pushing back as hard.

## FAQs about the Junior Superfly 11 Academy AG

### What's the difference between artificial grass and firm ground cleats?

Firm ground cleats use fewer, longer studs for natural grass. These use shorter hollowed studs built for synthetic pitches.

### Can these work on natural grass?

Occasionally. If most of the season is on real grass, buy firm ground instead.

### How do I know when to size up?

Check toe room every few weeks. Less than a finger's width means it's time.
