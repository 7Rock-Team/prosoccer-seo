## FAQs about the F50 Hyperfast League Indoor

### Can these be worn on turf?

No. The flat outsole needs a hard court. On turf there's nothing for the tread to bite, so choose a turf model.

### How does the League compare to the Pro indoor?

Same flat-court idea, different build. The Pro uses a different upper and adds a Lightstrike midsole.

### Will they mark a school gym floor?

No. The rubber on the Chaos vs Control League Indoor is non-marking, which is what almost every facility requires.
