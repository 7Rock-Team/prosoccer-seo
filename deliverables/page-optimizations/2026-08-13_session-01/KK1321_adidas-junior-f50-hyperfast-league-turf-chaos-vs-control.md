## Fit Notes

These run regular through the midfoot. Buy the size they wear now, not the size you hope they grow into. A shoe that's too long slides at the heel and costs them touches. Leave a thumb's width at the toe and adjust the lacing as feet change.

## Care and Maintenance

- Brush off turf pellets and dirt after every session
- Air-dry away from heaters and direct sun
- Stuff with newspaper overnight to pull moisture and hold shape
- Keep them out of the washer and dryer
