## Kangaroo leather stops being a material and starts being a fit

K-Leather is thin for its strength, which is how it goes soft without going sloppy. Wear it and the upper takes the shape of your foot, the ridge of the joint, the width across the forefoot. A synthetic upper keeps its own shape and asks you to fit it. ## Fit Notes

Morelia runs narrow through the forefoot by design, so a wide foot should try a pair on first. True to size for most players. The leather gives over the first few weeks, so don't size up hoping the gap fills in.

## Care and Maintenance

- Brush off dried grass and dirt before the leather hardens
- Air-dry away from heat, sun, and dryers
- Stuff with newspaper to hold shape and pull out moisture
- Condition the kangaroo leather every few weeks in season
- Never machine wash; heat and detergent strip real leather

## FAQs about the Morelia Neo V Beta Made in Japan

### How long is break-in, and how does K-Leather mold to your foot?

Short. The leather starts giving inside the first few sessions and settles within two or three weeks. It stretches where your foot is widest and holds everywhere else, so the fit ends up shaped to you.

### What separates this from the Beta Elite?

Material and build, not construction tier. Beta Elite runs a synthetic upper. This one is kangaroo leather, made in Japan, on the 24 hour lasting.

### Can I wear these on artificial grass?

The plate is firm ground, built for natural grass. Artificial surfaces load the studs differently and scuff leather faster.
