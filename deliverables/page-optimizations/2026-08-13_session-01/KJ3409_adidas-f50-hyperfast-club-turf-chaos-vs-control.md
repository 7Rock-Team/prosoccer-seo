Turf punishes the wrong shoe. Firm-ground studs sink and stick where the fibers are short, and running shoes slide. ## Fit Notes

Order their usual size. Speed shoes like the F50 hug the forefoot, so a wide-footed player is usually happier a half size up. Mid-growth-spurt, leave a thumb's width at the toe.

## Care and Maintenance

- Knock the turf pellets out after every session
- Wipe the synthetic upper with a damp cloth; no leather, so skip the conditioner
- Air-dry at room temperature, away from heaters and direct sun
- Stuff with paper overnight to hold the shape

## FAQs about the F50 Hyperfast Club Turf

### What surface are these made for?
Short synthetic grass and older astroturf, the fields most club practices and small-sided leagues use. Natural grass calls for studs.

### How is this different from the Club Mid?
Same Club tier and same pack, different surface. This is the low turf shoe; the Mid is the firm and multi-ground version.

### Can they wear these indoors?
No. A turf outsole is built for synthetic grass, and most indoor leagues require a flat non-marking sole.
