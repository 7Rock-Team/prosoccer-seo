## Fit Notes

Regular fit through the forefoot, so take your usual adidas size. The lacing pulls the Halocage+ down tight on a lower-volume foot, or eases off if you're wide. Indoor models sit closer to the floor than outdoor pairs, so first wear feels snug.

## Care and Maintenance

- Wipe the outsole dry after each session so the rubber keeps its bite
- Spot-clean the Haloshell+ mesh with lukewarm water and mild soap, never a machine wash
- Air-dry at room temperature, away from heat above 105°F (40°C)
- Loosen the laces before pulling them off so the Halocage+ holds its shape
- Air them out after play so the synthetic lining dries
