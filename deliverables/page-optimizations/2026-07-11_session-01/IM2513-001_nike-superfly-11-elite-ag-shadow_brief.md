## Fit Notes
The FlyWeave Ultra runs snug and tailored, so most players get a true-to-size fit. The collar sits high; if you've never worn a high-cut cleat, give it a wear or two to settle.

## Care and Maintenance
- Air-dry after every match and stuff with newspaper to hold the shape
- Keep them out of heat, sun, and the dryer, which warps the plate
- Brush off dried grass and turf pellets before storing
- Wipe the synthetic upper with a damp cloth; no conditioner needed

## FAQs about the Superfly 11 Elite AG

### Does the Superfly 11 Elite AG run true to size?
It runs true to size for most players. The woven upper fits snug, so narrow feet are well covered; wider feet may want to try first.

### What's the difference between the Superfly and the Vapor?
The Superfly is high-cut for straight-line speed. The Vapor is low-cut for agility and close control.

### Can I wear these on natural grass?
This is the AG version, tuned for artificial grass. On natural grass you'd want the FG plate instead.

### What is ZoomX and why does it matter?
ZoomX is Nike's highest-energy-return foam, borrowed from their record-chasing running shoes. It holds your pace through a full run.
