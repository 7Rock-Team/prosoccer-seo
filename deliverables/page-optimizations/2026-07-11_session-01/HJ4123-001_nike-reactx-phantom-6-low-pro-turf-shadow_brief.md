## Fit Notes
Nike reworked the frame for a more natural fit, most noticeable in the toe box. True to size for most; if you run narrow or want a snug lock, try a half size down.

## Care and Maintenance
- Brush off turf pellets and dirt before storing
- Air-dry after every session; stuff with newspaper to hold shape
- Keep them off direct heat, sun, and dryers, which warp the outsole

## FAQs about the Phantom 6 Low Pro Turf

### What surface are these built for?
Turf: artificial and older astroturf, so cages and small-sided games. For natural grass, choose a firm-ground plate.

### What does the ReactX foam do?
It cushions each step and returns energy, so the hard court beats up your legs less over a full session.

### Is this the same upper as the Phantom 6 Elite?
No. The Elite uses Gripknit; this Pro turf shoe uses VNMSkin with Flyknit, the same control-first touch for less.
