## Fit Notes
The Vapor 17 Pro runs true to size for most. The Flyknit hugs the foot, so between sizes or wider, size up a half. Snug is the point, but don't jam your toes at the wall.

## Care and Maintenance
- Brush off turf pellets and dirt before storing
- Air-dry away from direct heat; stuff with newspaper
- Skip the dryer and direct sun, which warp the outsole

## FAQs about the Vapor 17 Pro Turf

### What surface are these made for?
Shorter synthetic turf: older astroturf, cages, indoor fields, weeknight 5s. For grass, choose the FG version.

### How is the Pro different from the Elite?
The Pro uses Flyknit for a close touch; the Elite steps up to Atomknit at a higher price.

### Do they run true to size?
Yes, for most feet. The Flyknit wraps close, so between sizes or wide, go up a half.
