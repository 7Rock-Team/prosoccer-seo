## Fit Notes
The Maestro fits close and glove-like, so most go true to size. The TECHLEATHER gives as it warms, so size down if you want it snug; wider feet hold their size and let it break in.

## Care and Maintenance
- Air-dry after every match; stuff with newspaper to hold shape
- Keep them off direct heat, sun, and dryers, which warp the plate
- Brush off grass and dirt; no leather conditioner needed on the synthetic upper

## FAQs about the Tiempo Maestro Elite FG

### Is the Tiempo Maestro Elite real leather?
No. It's TECHLEATHER, a supersoft synthetic that holds a more consistent touch than natural leather, especially in the wet.

### Does the Maestro Elite run true to size?
For most players, yes. For a very close fit or a narrow foot, size down a half; wider feet stay true.

### What surface are these cleats for?
Firm ground: dry, natural grass. For artificial grass or soft pitches, choose an AG or SG plate instead.

### What is the Maestro360 plate?
A split plate that separates forefoot from heel, so the upper wraps your arch for a close, connected feel when turning.
