## Care and Maintenance
- Air-dry after every match; stuff with newspaper to hold shape and pull moisture
- Keep them off direct heat, sun, and dryers, which break down the knit and warp the plate
- Brush off dried turf pellets before they set in
- Clear the hollowed studs after turf sessions so they keep their bite

## FAQs about the Vapor 17 Elite AG

### Can I wear these on natural grass?
They're built for artificial grass. The hollowed AG studs bite harder synthetic, so they're less ideal on soft natural pitches. For natural grass, the firm-ground (FG) version is the better call.

### Do the Vapor 17 Elite AG run true to size?
For most players, yes. The Atomknit upper wraps close. Between sizes, stay true rather than sizing up.

### What makes the AG plate different from firm ground?
Hollowed studs plus extra forefoot studs. That spreads your weight across the synthetic and keeps you planted through cuts, where a firm-ground plate sits high and slips.

### What's the Shadow Pack colorway?
A full blackout, Black/Black/Illusion Green, from Nike's FA26 Shadow Pack.
