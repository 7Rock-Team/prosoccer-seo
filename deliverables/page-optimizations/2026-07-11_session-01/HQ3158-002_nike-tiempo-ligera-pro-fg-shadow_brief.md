## The leather feel, minus the leather weight

Ligera means light in Spanish, and that's the idea. You get the classic Tiempo touch, the way the ball settles when you cushion it out of the air, without the heavy k-leather cleat your feet remember. Think of it as a leather jacket cut down to a windbreaker: the feel you buy leather for stays, the bulk that slowed you down is gone.

## What's under the blackout

The upper is stitched TECHLEATHER, Nike's engineered leather. It's supersoft on contact and holds a steadier touch in the wet than natural leather, so a soaked pitch feels like a dry one, and it molds to your foot without overstretching. Underneath is a traditional full-plate with conical studs. If you've worn the Maestro and its Maestro360 split plate, this is the simpler feel: one solid plate that plants and turns on dry grass. See the full [Nike Tiempo line](https://www.prosoccer.com/collections/nike-tiempo).

## Who it's for and where to wear them

This one's for the touch-oriented midfielder who wants to feel the ball, not just strike it. If you play on dry, firm natural grass, the Ligera Pro gives leather control at Pro-tier value, in a clean [Nike Shadow Pack](https://www.prosoccer.com/collections/nike-shadow-soccer-cleats) blackout.

## Fit Notes
Most players find the Tiempo true to size. If you're between sizes or run wide, size up half a step; the leather gives a little as it breaks in.

## Care and Maintenance
- Air-dry after each match; stuff with newspaper to hold shape
- Keep away from heat, sun, and dryers, which dry the leather and warp the plate
- Brush off dried grass and dirt before storing
- Condition the TECHLEATHER lightly every few weeks in season

## FAQs about the Tiempo Ligera Pro FG

### How is the Ligera different from the Tiempo Maestro?
The plate and the price. The Ligera runs a traditional full-plate for a simpler feel, where the Maestro uses the Maestro360 split plate, and it sits at a lighter Pro-tier price.

### Is TECHLEATHER real leather?
It's Nike's engineered leather, not natural k-leather. You get the soft, cushioned touch, plus a steadier feel in the wet.

### What surface are these built for?
Firm, dry natural grass. For wet, soft, or artificial surfaces, pick a different plate.
