## The Three Adidas Soccer Cleats Silos and How to Pick

Adidas soccer cleats sit inside three silos in 2026: Predator, F50, and Copa Pure. Each one is built for a different game. Predator is the control player's boot, for the midfielder who runs the tempo and strikes with spin. F50 is the speed boot, knit upper, lightweight chassis, for the winger who beats the back line on the burst. Copa is the leather boot, calfskin upper, soft touch, for the player whose first touch settles the ball. Adidas used to run a fourth silo, the X; the 2024 F50 relaunch absorbed that role.

## Predator 26: What Bellingham Wears

The Predator 26 launched late 2025. NANOSTRIKE+ upper, return of POWERSPINE through the midfoot, $260-280 in the Elite build, dropping through League ($100) and Club tiers. The line has belonged to the control player since Craig Johnston designed the original in 1994. Beckham, Zidane, Gerrard, Kaká, every Predator generation has been worn by the player who runs the game from the middle. Jude Bellingham anchors the current signature at Real Madrid; Pedri at Barcelona, Declan Rice at Arsenal. If your game is the through ball and the curling shot from the top of the box, the Predator 26 is your line. The [full Predator lineup](https://www.prosoccer.com/collections/adidas-predator) carries every tier.

## F50 in 2026: Messi, Hora Dorada, and the Speed Game

The F50 came back in 2024 after a decade off the market and took the speed-boot title back. Lionel Messi wears the signature for Inter Miami and Argentina; the Hora Dorada Pack is the spring 2026 colorway, gold and iridescent on the upper. Elite tier runs HybridTouch upper with a stiffened SPRINTFRAME chassis. Pro and League tiers carry the same silhouette at lower prices. The F50 is the adidas soccer cleat for the player whose game lives behind the back line: the winger making the run, the striker stretching the keeper, the full-back overlapping on the touchline.

## Copa Pure IV: The Touch Boot

The Copa line started in 1979 with the Copa Mundial, still at $180 forty-six years later. The current generation is the Copa Pure IV, launched 2026 with the Fusionskin upper: calfskin forefoot for leather touch, mesh midfoot for lockdown. Calfskin gets most of the comfort and ball feel at a different price point than kangaroo. If your game is the touch-and-turn in the box and the held-up ball with your back to goal, the Copa Pure IV is your line.

## How to Pick Your Tier and Plate

Each adidas soccer cleats silo runs four tiers: Elite at the top (pro-spec, K-leather or HybridTouch), Pro below it, League the mid-tier most club players pick, Club at entry. The plate matches the surface: FG for natural grass, AG for third-generation turf, MG for both, TF for older astroturf, IC for futsal. Pick your silo for your game, your tier for your budget, your plate for your field. The [full footwear catalog](https://www.prosoccer.com/collections/all-footwear) carries every adidas soccer cleat across every plate.
