## How adidas Designed the 2026 Away Jerseys

Away kits are where federation kit designers take the bigger swings. Home kits carry the constraint of national colors; away kits get to interpret heritage through a wider lens. **Mexico's** white base carries a Grecas pattern, the geometric stepped motif from Aztec architecture, with "SOMOS MEXICO" inscribed at the back of the neck. **Argentina's** away unifies the three different blue shades from the country's three World Cup wins (1978, 1986, 2022) into one shirt, a tribute to the Albiceleste's full trophy lineage with the AFA three-stars patch above the crest. **Germany's** away is collegiate navy with aqua accents, the final adidas kit Germany will ever wear before the federation switches to Nike in 2027 after a 70-year partnership. **Japan's** drop carries a similar deep navy with technical patterning that nods to the federation's craft tradition. **Italy's** Azzurri away, **Spain's** Lamine Yamal generation kit, **Colombia's** ode to the country's Pacific and Atlantic coastlines, **Belgium's** Red Devils change shirt, and **Sweden's** Tre Kronor cool-toned third all carry the same Trefoil DNA with their own federation accents.
