## Why Soccer Scarves Are the Supporter's Original Kit

Before replica jerseys existed, fans showed allegiance through ribbons, rosettes, and wooden rattles. The soccer scarf arrived in the early 1900s on the terraces of Sunderland, Manchester, Glasgow, and Liverpool, where supporters needed a way to stay warm in the wind and rain without losing the color of their club. Desmond Morris named it in The Soccer Tribe: scarves "owe their origins to the need to keep warm on the freezing terraces of the damp and draughty British stadia." A century later the scarf still does the work no other piece of kit does. You can wear a jersey to the supermarket. You hold a scarf aloft during the national anthem. Scarves go up when goals are scored, they wrap around the necks of supporters in the away end, they get raised in unison during You'll Never Walk Alone at Anfield and during the Marcha de la Bersagliera at Italy matches. The scarf is the only piece of fan kit designed to be held, not just worn.

## The Scarf Wall: How Soccer Crowds Use This Piece of Kit

The scarf wall is a tradition. Supporters raise their scarves above their heads horizontally during the national anthem, during pre-match chants, during the moments before kickoff when the camera pans across the home end. The result is a block of pure color, a federation's flag turned into a living thing. Argentina supporters do it at every World Cup match they show up to. Mexico supporters fill the Rose Bowl with green when El Tri plays a friendly. USMNT supporters did it at Estadio Akron in 2022 and will do it in 2026 in Seattle, Kansas City, LA, and Inglewood. The scarf goes up during goals as well. Some federations have specific scarf traditions: the Italian crowd raises azzurro scarves for Notti Magiche, the Argentine crowd holds the albiceleste up for the national anthem until the line "ved en trono a la noble igualdad," the Mexican crowd raises verde for the federation's pre-match Cielito Lindo. A scarf is the only piece of national team kit designed for this. A jersey can't do it.
