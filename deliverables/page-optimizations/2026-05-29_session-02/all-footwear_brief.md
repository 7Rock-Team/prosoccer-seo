## Soccer Cleats by the Three Big Silos

Nike, adidas, and Puma run the soccer cleat world. Nike splits three ways: Mercurial for speed (Vapor 16, Superfly 10, Mbappe and Vinicius), Tiempo for the leather touch (Legend 10, Maestro), Phantom for precision (Phantom 6, Bellingham for England). Adidas keeps three: Predator for control (Predator 26, Bellingham at Real Madrid), F50 for speed (Messi for Inter Miami), Copa Pure for first touch (Copa Pure IV, Copa Mundial still at $180 forty-six years on). Puma runs King for comfort, Future for dribbling, Ultra for pace. Below them: Mizuno Morelia II, New Balance Furon, Joma and Charly across Liga MX, Sokito plant-based.

## Picking Your Plate: Firm Ground, Turf, Indoor, and the Rest

The plate underneath the soccer cleat has to match the surface. Firm Ground (FG) is for natural grass, what most Saturday rec leagues play on; the firm ground soccer cleats lineup runs deepest. Artificial Grass (AG) is for green-plastic third-generation turf, shorter studs, won't tear up the surface or your knees. Soft Ground (SG) is for wet grass with removable metal studs, a European pro thing. Multi-Ground (MG) works on FG and AG. Turf (TF) carries dozens of rubber studs across the outsole. Indoor is flat-soled for futsal and gym floors. The wrong plate on the wrong surface is the fastest way to roll an ankle.

## Picking by Position and by Player

Strikers and wingers lean speed: Mercurial, F50, Ultra, New Balance Furon. Attacking midfielders split between control and touch: Phantom or Predator for the through ball, Tiempo or Copa or King for the first touch. Defensive midfielders and center-backs live in the control silos. Outside backs lean speed. Goalkeepers wear silo-specific keeper editions with grip built for distribution.

## The Tier Ladder and the Kids Cut

Every silo runs a price ladder. Elite is the pro spec, K-leather or HybridTouch upper, $250 to $300. Pro sits just below at $180 to $220. League is where most club players actually buy: synthetic upper, 80 percent of the feel for half the cost, $80 to $120. Club is the entry tier, $50 to $80. Kids soccer cleats run the same silos in junior cuts. The right silo at the League tier beats the wrong silo at the Elite tier.
