Nike stitches Techleather onto the upper right where your foot meets the ball. It's supersoft, and it holds a more consistent touch in wet or dry conditions than natural leather does. ## Care and Maintenance

- Brush off dried grass and dirt before storing
- Air-dry at room temperature, stuffed with newspaper
- No dryers or radiators, heat warps the plate
- Wipe the Techleather with a damp cloth

## FAQs about the Tiempo Ligera Pro FG

### Does the Techleather upper need conditioner?

No. Techleather is synthetic, so it won't dry out the way natural leather does. A damp cloth and air-drying covers it.

### Can I wear these on artificial grass?

They're built for firm natural ground. If synthetic surfaces dominate your week, the turf build is the smarter buy.

### Do they run true to size?

Yes for most players. The upper molds with wear, so between sizes, take the smaller one.
