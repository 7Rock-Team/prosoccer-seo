## FAQs about the adidas Predator Club Turf

### Can I wear these on natural grass?

You can, but the rubber turf outsole is made for artificial surfaces. On soft natural grass you'll want studs.

### How is Club different from the higher Predator tiers?

Club is the entry tier: the soft synthetic upper, the textured finish and the laced floating tongue, without the extra hardware the upper tiers carry.

### Will these work indoors?

Not really. The turf pattern is built for outdoor artificial grass; a flat court shoe suits a gym floor better.
