## FAQs about the Predator Elite AG

### Should I get the AG or the FG version of the Predator Elite?

Pick the surface you play on most. The AG plate carries shorter, more numerous studs made to grip artificial grass and spare your joints on the firmer synthetic; the firm-ground plate suits natural grass and wears faster on plastic. If your league plays 3G or 4G turf, the AG is the right call.

### What does the fold-over tongue actually do?

It folds across your laces and tucks them away, leaving one clean surface where you strike the ball. No lace bumps underfoot means a truer contact on passes and shots. It's a longtime Predator signature, and it fits the control-first build of this cleat.

### Is the Predator Elite good for a control player?

That's exactly who the adidas Predator Elite AG is for. The Nanostrike+ grip zone, the steady Powerspine midfoot, and the clean fold-over striking surface all serve the player who wins games with touch and a well-struck ball over raw pace.

### Do these run true to size?

Yes, the Predator Elite runs true to size for most players in a snug regular fit. Between sizes, the laces let you adjust; wide feet are usually fine taking true size.

### Can I wear these on natural grass too?

In a pinch, but the AG studs are tuned for synthetic surfaces. For regular natural-grass play the firm-ground version grips better. Match the plate to your main surface and they'll last longer.
