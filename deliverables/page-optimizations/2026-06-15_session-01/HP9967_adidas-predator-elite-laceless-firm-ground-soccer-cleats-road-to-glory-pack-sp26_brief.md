## Fit Notes

Laceless cleats fit closer than laced ones because the knit holds your foot. The Predator Elite Laceless runs true to size for most players. Wider feet or high insteps may want a half size up while the Primeknit collar settles. If you sit between sizes, go up rather than down; you cannot open a laceless cleat the way you ease a lace.

## Care and Maintenance

- Air-dry after every match; stuff with newspaper to draw out moisture and hold the collar's shape
- Keep them off direct heat, dryers, and a hot car; store around 70°F (21°C)
- Brush dried grass and dirt off the Nanostrike+ mesh before storing
- Synthetic upper, so no conditioner needed; a damp cloth keeps it clean
- Ease the collar on and off gently to keep the Primeknit from stretching

## FAQs about the Predator Elite Laceless

### Why choose the laceless Elite over the laced Predator Elite?

Pick the laceless if you want one clean, unbroken surface where you strike the ball. The laced Elite gives you on-the-fly tightness adjustment; the laceless trades that for a smoother contact patch and a sock-like collar that locks the foot through the knit instead of a lace. If your game is built on the quality of your touch and your striking, the laceless is the one.

### Do laceless cleats stay secure without a lace?

Yes. The engineered Primeknit collar and the snug knit upper hold the foot in place, and the Powerspine keeps the midfoot stable through hard cuts and shots. Most players find the lockdown feels more uniform than a laced cleat because there is no pressure point along the laces.

### What surface is the Predator Elite Laceless made for?

Firm natural grass. The Strikeframe plate and stud pattern are built for dry, firm pitches. For artificial grass or soft, wet ground, look at the AG or SG options in the Predator line instead.

### How should the laceless fit compared to a laced cleat?

Closer and more sock-like. They run true to size for most feet. Wider feet or high insteps may prefer a half size up, since there is no lace to open up. When in doubt, size up rather than down.

### Is the Road to Glory Pack colorway the same cleat as the standard Elite Laceless?

Yes, same Predator Elite Laceless construction underneath. The Road to Glory Pack is the Solar Turbo, Thermal Chrome, and Core Black colorway treatment; the Nanostrike+ strike zone, Primeknit collar, Powerspine, and Strikeframe plate are unchanged.
