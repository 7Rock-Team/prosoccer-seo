The F50 League runs true to size, so order your normal soccer shoe size. The Haloskin upper hugs the foot without a long break-in. Wider feet can go up a half size without losing the close wrap.

## Care and Maintenance

- Air-dry after every session; stuff with newspaper to pull moisture and hold the shape
- Keep them away from direct heat, sunlight, and dryers, which break down the synthetic upper
- Brush off court dust, then wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap when needed
- Store somewhere cool and dry, not in a hot gym bag overnight

## FAQs about the F50 League Indoor

### Do the F50 League indoor shoes run true to size?
They run true to size with a regular fit, so order your normal soccer shoe size. If you have a wider foot or like a little more room up front, going up a half size works without losing the close Haloskin wrap.

### What surface are these built for?
Flat indoor courts: gym floors, futsal courts, and sports halls. The non-marking rubber outsole grips smooth indoor surfaces and won't leave marks on the boards. They're not built for grass or turf, which need a studded or turf outsole.

### How is the League tier different from the Pro indoor version?
The League tier is the value build for the regular indoor player. It carries the same F50 speed shape and non-marking outsole, but trades the priciest pro-level materials for a season-friendly price. The Pro indoor targets the higher-level competitor who wants the top-end upper.

### Are these good for adult league and futsal?
Yes. They're aimed squarely at the adult indoor player: weeknight leagues, futsal runs, college rec games on the gym floor. Light off the dribble, grippy on flat courts, and tough enough to keep showing up week after week.

### What's the Road to Glory pack colorway?
