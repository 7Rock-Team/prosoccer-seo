## FAQs about the Junior F50 League Turf

### Why turf shoes instead of regular cleats on turf?
Firm-ground cleats have long studs made to dig into natural grass. On turf those studs sit on top of the surface and slide, which is uncomfortable and can roll an ankle. The Junior F50 League Turf uses a flat rubber outsole with a grip pattern made for short synthetic blades, so your kid stays planted through cuts and tackles.

### Can my kid wear these on grass too?
They'll work for a casual kickaround on grass, but they're built for turf and won't grip natural grass the way real cleats do. If your child plays league matches on grass, get a firm-ground pair for those. Think of this as the turf-specific shoe in the bag.

### What does the EVA midsole actually do?
It's the cushioning layer underfoot. Turf is harder and more abrasive than grass, and kids spend a lot of hours on it. The lightweight EVA midsole softens each step so their feet aren't beat up after a long practice plus a game.

### Are these going to hold up?
The turf-specific rubber outsole is made for the abrasive synthetic surface, so it wears far better there than cleats would. Air-drying them after sessions and brushing off the pellets (see Care and Maintenance) keeps them in shape through the season.

### How do I size them?
They run true to size for most kids and start at US 1.5. If your child is in a growth spurt, a half size up gives a little room while the laces take up the slack early on.
