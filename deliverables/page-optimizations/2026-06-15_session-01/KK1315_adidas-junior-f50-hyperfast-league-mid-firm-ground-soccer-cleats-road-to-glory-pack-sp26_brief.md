## FAQs about the Junior F50 League Mid

### What does the mid-cut collar do?
The collar rises just above the ankle bone and closes snug around it, so the cleat moves with the foot instead of letting it slide around inside the shoe. Most kids describe it as feeling held rather than loose, which helps when they're planting and turning at speed.

### Does the collar actually support the ankle?
It adds coverage and a snug, held-in fit around the ankle, and many parents like that for a younger player still learning to cut and pivot. It's a fit-and-feel benefit, not a medical brace, so it's not a substitute for a prescribed ankle support if your kid has had an injury. For everyday youth play, the snug collar gives a reassuring lockdown.

### How do I size a cleat with a collar?
Size the same way you would any cleat, by the foot, not the collar. These run true to size. If your player is between sizes, go up a half size, not a full one; the collar already keeps the ankle snug, so you don't want extra room in the toe.

### Are these good for turf or indoor?
No. This is a firm-ground build with studs made for dry natural grass. On turf or an indoor court the studs won't grip right and they'll wear faster. The adidas F50 collection carries turf and indoor versions for those surfaces.

### Will these hold up for a full season?
They're the League tier, built to take a youth season of grass play. Air-dry them after matches and brush off the dirt, and the synthetic upper and collar stay in good shape. Care matters more than tier for how long a kid's cleats last.
