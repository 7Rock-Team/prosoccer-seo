## FAQs about the Predator Elite FG

### Are the adidas Predator Elite FG laced or laceless?

These are the laced version. The Predator Elite FG uses a lace closure with a floating tongue, so you control the wrap and lockdown over the laces. adidas also makes a laceless Predator Elite for players who want a clean striking surface with no laces; this SKU is the laced build.

### Do the Predator Elite cleats run true to size?

For most players, yes. The adidas Predator Elite FG runs true to size with a snug regular forefoot. If you have a narrow foot or want a tighter feel, go down half a size.

### Can I wear these on artificial grass?

The FG plate is built for natural grass and well-maintained pitches. For dedicated artificial grass, the AG version with shorter, more numerous studs is the better choice and protects your knees and ankles on the firmer surface.

### What makes the Predator different from the adidas F50?

The Predator is a control-and-strike cleat: the Nanostrike+ grip elements help with precise passing and bent shots. The F50 is a speed cleat built around acceleration and a lightweight feel. Pick the Predator if your game runs on touch and precision; pick the F50 if it runs on pace.
