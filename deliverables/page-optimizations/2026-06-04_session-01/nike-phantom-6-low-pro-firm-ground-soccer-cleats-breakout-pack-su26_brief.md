## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**Does the Phantom 6 Low Pro run true to size?**
For most players, yes, with a close foot-molded fit through the toe box. If you have a wider foot or want a touch more room, go up a half size. The low cut feels broken in after a session or two.

**Should I get the FG or the AG Phantom 6 Low Pro?**
Pick FG for dry, natural grass, the surface most league and school games run on. Pick AG if you mostly play on artificial grass, since its studs are tuned for synthetic. Running FG studs on hard synthetic wears them down fast.

**What's the difference between the Pro and the Elite Phantom 6?**
The Pro uses VNMSkin with Flyknit for a grippy strike surface at a friendlier price. The Elite steps up to Gripknit, the stickier textured upper, and sits at the top of the line. Both share the Phantom's precision DNA; the Pro is the value pick.

**Is the Low Pro a good first serious cleat for a developing playmaker?**
Yes. It carries the Phantom's control-and-finishing feel, the grippy strike zone, and firm-ground studs at the accessible Pro-tier price, so a developing player gets real precision without paying for the Elite tier.
