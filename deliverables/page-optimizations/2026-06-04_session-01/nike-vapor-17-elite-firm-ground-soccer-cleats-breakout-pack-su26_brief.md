## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**Does the Vapor 17 Elite run true to size?**
For most players, yes, with a snug foot-molded fit and a low, racy toe box. If you have a wider foot or want a touch more room, go up a half size. The Atomknit feels tight the first session, then molds to your foot.

**Should I get the Vapor or the Superfly?**
Pick the Vapor if your game is the dribble and the cut in tight space; it's the low-cut, no-collar option for a free ankle and the lightest feel. Pick the Superfly if you sprint in open space and want the collared, locked-in ankle. Same Mercurial speed DNA, two different ankle feels.

**Should I get the FG or the AG Vapor 17?**
Pick FG for dry, natural grass, the surface most league and school games run on. Pick AG if you mostly play on artificial grass; its studs are tuned for synthetic. Running FG Chevron studs on hard synthetic wears them down fast.

**What makes the Elite tier different from the cheaper Vapors?**
The Elite uses the Atomknit upper and the full FlyLite plate, the lightest build in the Vapor 17 line. Lower tiers swap to heavier materials and simpler plates at a friendlier price, so they weigh a touch more and sit a step back from the ball.
