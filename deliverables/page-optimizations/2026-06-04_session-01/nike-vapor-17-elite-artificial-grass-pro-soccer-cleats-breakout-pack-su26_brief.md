## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**Does the Vapor 17 Elite run true to size?**
For most players, yes, with a snug Atomknit fit that hugs the foot. If you have a wider foot or want a touch more room, go up a half size. The upper feels tight the first session, then molds to your foot and settles.

**Should I get the AG or the FG Vapor 17?**
Pick AG if you mostly play on artificial grass, the green synthetic surface; its studs are hollowed and tuned for that deck. Pick FG for dry, natural grass. Running firm-ground studs on hard synthetic stings underfoot and wears the studs down fast.

**What's the difference between the Vapor and the Superfly?**
Both are Mercurial quickness cleats, but the Vapor is low-cut with no collar, built for the cut and close control in tight spaces. The Superfly adds a collar that wraps the ankle for open-space running. Pick the Vapor if your game is beating people off the dribble; pick the Superfly if it's running into space.

**What makes the Elite tier different from the lower tiers?**
The Elite uses the Atomknit upper, Nike's lightest Flyknit for the closest possible touch, on the FlyLite plate, and it sits at the top of the Vapor 17 line. Lower tiers swap in heavier materials and a firmer plate at a friendlier price.
