## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**Does the Superfly 11 Elite run true to size?**
For most players, yes, with a snug foot-hugging fit. If you have a wider foot or want a touch more room, go up a half size. The high collar feels tight the first session, then settles and disappears.

**Should I get the AG or the FG Superfly 11?**
Pick AG if you mostly play on artificial grass; the studs are shorter and tuned to grip synthetic without grabbing. Pick FG for dry natural grass, the surface most league and school games run on. Running AG studs on natural grass robs you of bite, and FG studs on turf wear down fast.

**What's the difference between the Superfly and the Vapor?**
The Superfly is the high-cut Mercurial with the Dynamic Fit collar wrapping your ankle. The Vapor is the low-cut version of the same speed silo, lighter at the ankle with no collar. Pick the Superfly if you want the locked-in ankle feel at top speed; pick the Vapor if you prefer a freer ankle.

**What does ZoomX foam do in a soccer cleat?**
ZoomX is Nike's highest-energy-return foam, carried over from its running line and used here for the first time in a soccer cleat. Paired with the Air Zoom forefoot unit, it gives you a springier push off the first step, which is the whole point for a player whose game is the sprint.
