## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**Does the Superfly 11 Elite run true to size?**
For most players, yes, with a snug foot-wrapping fit. If you have a wider foot or want a touch more room, go up a half size. The Dynamic Fit collar feels tight the first session, then settles in.

**Should I get the FG or the AG Superfly 11?**
Pick FG for dry, natural grass, the surface most league and school games run on. Pick AG if you mostly play on artificial grass; its studs are tuned for synthetic. Running FG studs on hard synthetic wears them down fast.

**What's the difference between the Superfly and the Vapor in the Mercurial line?**
The Superfly is the high-cut with the Dynamic Fit collar that wraps your ankle. The Vapor is the low-cut speed cleat with no collar. Pick the Superfly if you want the ankle locked in; pick the Vapor if you want a lighter, freer ankle.

**What is ZoomX and why does it matter here?**
ZoomX is Nike's highest-energy-return foam, the stuff that lives in their fastest running shoes. The Superfly 11 Elite is the first soccer cleat to carry it, paired with the Air Zoom forefoot unit, so more of the force you put into the ground comes back to push you forward.
