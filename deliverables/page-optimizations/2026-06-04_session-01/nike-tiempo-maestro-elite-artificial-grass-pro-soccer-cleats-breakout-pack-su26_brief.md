## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**Does the Tiempo Maestro Elite run true to size?**
For most players, yes, with a snug foot-hugging fit. The TECHLEATHER softens and molds over the first couple of sessions, so a close fit out of the box is normal. If you have a wider foot or want a touch more room, go up a half size.

**Should I get the AG or the FG Tiempo Maestro?**
Pick AG if you mostly play on artificial grass; the studs are hollowed and tuned for harder synthetic surfaces, with extra forefoot studs for grip. Pick FG for dry natural grass. Running AG studs on natural firm ground, or FG studs on hard synthetic, costs you traction and wears the plate down faster.

**Can I wear the Tiempo Maestro Elite AG on turf or indoor courts?**
No. The AG plate is for longer-pile artificial grass, not short-pile turf (TF) or flat indoor courts (IC). Studded plates on hard flat surfaces give you no real grip and beat up your feet. Match the plate to the surface.

**What makes TECHLEATHER different from real leather?**
TECHLEATHER is a supersoft synthetic that gives you the touch and feel players love about natural leather without soaking up water, so the ball behaves the same wet or dry. The molded touch lines add grip you don't get from a plain upper.
