## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**Does the Phantom 6 High Elite AG run true to size?**
For most players, yes, with a snug foot-molded fit. If you have a wider foot or want a touch more room, go up a half size. The Dynamic Fit collar feels tight the first session, then settles.

**Should I get the AG or the FG Phantom 6?**
Pick AG if you mostly play on artificial grass; its conical studs spread your weight across the synthetic surface and brake cleanly. Pick FG for dry, natural grass. Running FG studs on hard synthetic wears them down fast and can grab.

**What's the difference between the High and the Low Phantom 6?**
The High adds the Dynamic Fit collar that wraps your ankle plus the Ghost Lacing system over the laces. The Low is a clean low-cut with no collar. Pick the High if you want the locked-in ankle feel; pick the Low if you prefer a lighter, freer ankle.

**What makes the Elite tier different from the Pro?**
The Elite uses Gripknit, the sticky textured upper for maximum grip on the ball, and sits at the top of the Phantom 6 line. The Pro uses VNMSkin with Flyknit at a friendlier price.
