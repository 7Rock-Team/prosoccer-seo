## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**Does the Tiempo Maestro Elite run true to size?**
For most players, yes, with a snug foot-molded fit through the midfoot. If you have a wider foot or want a touch more room, go up a half size. The Maestro360 plate wraps the arch, so the cleat feels tight the first session, then settles.

**Should I get the FG or the AG Tiempo Maestro?**
Pick FG for dry, natural grass, the surface most league and school games run on. Pick AG if you mostly play on artificial grass; its studs are tuned for synthetic. Running FG studs on hard artificial turf wears them down fast and beats up your feet.

**How is TECHLEATHER different from the old Tiempo leather?**
TECHLEATHER is a supersoft synthetic with touch lines molded into the upper. It gives you a more consistent touch than natural leather when the grass is wet, it doesn't soak up water and get heavy, and it holds its shape over a season. The classic Tiempo feel, built to behave the same in any conditions.

**Is the Maestro a midfielder's cleat?**
That's the idea. The Tiempo Maestro is the control-and-passing cleat in Nike's lineup, built for the player who takes the ball under pressure and sets the tempo. If your game is touch, the half-turn, and the pass that splits the press, this is the one.
