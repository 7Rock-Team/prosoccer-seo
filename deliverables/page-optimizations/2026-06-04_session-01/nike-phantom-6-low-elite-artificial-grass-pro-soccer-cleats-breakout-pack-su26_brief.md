## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**Does the Phantom 6 Low Elite run true to size?**
For most players, yes, with a snug foot-molded fit. If you have a wider foot or want a touch more room, go up a half size. The Low cut means no collar to break in, so the ankle feels free right away.

**Should I get the AG or the FG Phantom 6?**
Pick AG if you mostly play on artificial grass; the conical studs are tuned for synthetic turf. Pick FG for dry, natural grass. Running AG studs on firm natural ground gives you less bite, and running FG studs on hard synthetic wears them down fast.

**What's the difference between the Low and the High Phantom 6?**
The Low is a clean low-cut with no collar, so the ankle stays free and quick. The High adds a Dynamic Fit collar that wraps the ankle plus a lacing cover over the laces. Pick the Low if you want a lighter, freer ankle; pick the High if you want the locked-in feel.

**What makes the Elite tier different from the Pro?**
The Elite uses Gripknit, the sticky textured upper for maximum grip on the ball, and sits at the top of the Phantom 6 line. The Pro uses a friendlier upper at a friendlier price.
