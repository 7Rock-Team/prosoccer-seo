## Fit Notes
Morelia leather runs on the snug side and breaks in to your foot over the first few wears. If you're between sizes or have a wider foot, go up a half size and let the K-leather give.

## Care and Maintenance
- Air-dry after every match and stuff with newspaper to hold the shape
- Keep them away from direct heat, sunlight, and dryers, which dry out the leather
- Brush off grass and dirt with a soft brush before storing
- Condition the K-leather every few weeks in season to keep it supple

### Does the Morelia Neo V Beta Elite run true to size?

### What's the difference between the Beta Elite and the Beta Pro?
