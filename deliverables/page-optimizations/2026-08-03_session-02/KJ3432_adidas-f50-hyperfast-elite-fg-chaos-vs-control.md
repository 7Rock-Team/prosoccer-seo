## Care and Maintenance
- Air-dry after every match, and stuff them with newspaper to hold shape and pull out moisture
- Keep them off direct heat, sunlight, and the dryer, all of which warp the plate and dry out the upper
- Brush off dried grass and dirt before storing
- The Haloskin+ synthetic upper needs only a soft brush and a damp wipe, no leather conditioner
- Store somewhere cool and dry, not a hot car or a sealed bag
