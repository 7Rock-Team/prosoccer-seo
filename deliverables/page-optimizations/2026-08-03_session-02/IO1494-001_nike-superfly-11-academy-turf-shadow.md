## Quick feet, close ball, turf under your studs
The turf game rewards the player who can knock the ball into a yard of space and get there first. The Nike Superfly 11 Academy is built for exactly that. Its lightweight NikeSkin upper sits close to the foot, so the ball stays with you when you push it into space and chase. It's part of Nike's [Mercurial speed line](https://www.prosoccer.com/collections/nike-mercurial), built to the entry-friendly Academy tier. Shadow Pack colors, Black and Illusion Green.

## Locked in for the cut
A snug Dynamic Fit collar wraps the ankle and holds your foot steady, so nothing shifts when you plant and change direction. Underneath, a rubber outsole made for turf and 3G bites into short synthetic surfaces and hard courts. If your matches are on grass instead, the [firm-ground and multi-ground version](https://www.prosoccer.com/products/nike-superfly-11-academy-fg-mg-soccer-cleats-shadow-fa26) covers that surface.

## Fit Notes
The Superfly 11 Academy Turf runs true to size with a snug midfoot. Players between sizes or with wider feet may want to size up a half.

## Care and Maintenance
- Knock off loose turf pellets and dirt after every session
- Wipe the upper with a damp cloth and mild soap, then air dry
- Keep them away from direct heat, which can warp the outsole
- Store in a ventilated bag, never sealed

## FAQs about the Superfly 11 Academy Turf
### Are these good for artificial grass and 3G?
Yes. The rubber outsole is built for turf and 3G, giving grip on short synthetic surfaces and hard courts where studded shoes don't belong.
### What does the Dynamic Fit collar do?
It's a snug, sock-like collar that wraps your ankle for a locked-in feel, so the shoe moves with you when you cut and accelerate.
### How do they fit?
