## Fit Notes
Mizuno leather runs snug and molds to the foot as it breaks in, so most players take their true size. If you are between sizes, size up a half. Wide feet should allow a short break-in window for the K-leather to give.

## Care and Maintenance
- Wipe off grass and dirt after every match with a soft, damp cloth
- Let them air dry away from direct heat, never near a radiator or dryer
- Condition the K-leather now and then to keep it supple and water-resistant
- Store with a shoe tree or loose paper to hold their shape
