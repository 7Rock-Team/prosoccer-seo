## Fit Notes

The Dynamic Fit collar is snug by design, so pull the cleat on over your heel and let the Flyknit settle. Wider feet get more room from the new toe box.

## Care and Maintenance

- Air-dry after every match; stuff with newspaper to hold shape and soak up moisture
- Keep them out of direct heat, sun, and dryers, which warp the plate and break down the mesh
- Brush off dried grass and dirt before storing

The high cut adds the Dynamic Fit collar for ankle lock-in; the low cut leaves it off for a freer feel.
