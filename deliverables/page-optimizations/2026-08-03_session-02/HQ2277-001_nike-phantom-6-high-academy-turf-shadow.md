## Built for the weeknight turf game
The Nike Phantom 6 High Academy Turf is built for the surface you play on most: the 3G pitch, the hard court, the small-sided game that runs late. A rubber turf outsole spreads short studs across the sole for grip and quick release on artificial ground. ReactX foam sits underneath and returns about 13% more energy than the older React setup, so the tenth sprint feels sharp.

## A high collar that locks the ankle in
The Dynamic Fit collar wraps the ankle in soft, stretchy Flyknit for a snug, sock-like hold through hard turns. An engineered mesh upper adds an expanded NikeSkin touch zone across the strike area, so the ball sits clean on your laces. Prefer firm ground? The [High Academy FG/MG sibling](https://www.prosoccer.com/products/nike-phantom-6-high-academy-fg-mg-soccer-cleats-shadow-fa26) runs the same collar on a molded plate.

## Care and Maintenance
- Knock off turf pellets and grit after every session
- Wipe with a damp cloth; skip the washing machine
- Air dry away from direct heat
- Store stuffed with loose paper to hold the shape
