## Care and Maintenance
- Wipe the leather clean with a damp cloth after play; keep them out of the washing machine
- Air dry away from direct heat to protect the upper
- Work in a little leather conditioner now and then to keep the FlyTouch upper soft
- Store with studs clean so grip stays sharp
