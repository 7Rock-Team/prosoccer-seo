## The control player who lives on turf

Your week isn't ninety minutes on a manicured pitch. It's small-sided sessions under the lights, 3G and 4G training surfaces, the cage on a Tuesday, a hard turf court where the ball moves quicker and so do you. That's where touch wins, and where most cleats give up the feel you need. The adidas Predator Pro turf shoe is built for the committed competitive player who calls that surface home.

Pro is the serious-amateur tier, one rung under the Elite. You get the Predator's control DNA tuned for hard ground, where the studs are short and many and your feet stay quick in tight spaces.

## Pro-tier control, turf-specific build

The Predator name has stood for one thing across three decades: putting more of the ball on your foot. This is the Pro reading of that idea, sized for turf. Nanostrike Pro layers an innovative mesh with integrated haptic areas across the strike zone, so the ball grips when you clip a pass in traffic or wrap your laces around a shot from the top of the box. Powerspine through the midfoot keeps the platform stable when you plant and turn on a hard surface, and Lightstrike cushioning underfoot gives you energy back over a long session. The fold-over tongue, standard at this tier, smooths the laces into one clean striking face.

The synthetic and textile upper sheds the scuffs that turf hands out, and a dense pattern of short studs on the synthetic outsole keeps you planted on 3G, 4G, and worn astroturf without the grab of a firm-ground plate.

## FAQs about the Predator Pro Turf

### What surface is the Predator Pro turf shoe built for?

It's a turf (TF) shoe, made for artificial turf and hard surfaces: 3G and 4G training pitches, small-sided cages, worn astroturf. The synthetic outsole uses a dense pattern of short studs instead of the longer studs you'd find on a firm-ground plate, which keeps your footing on hard ground.

### How does the Pro compare to the Predator Elite?

The Pro is the serious-amateur tier, one step below the Elite. It carries the same control idea, with Nanostrike Pro on the strike zone, Powerspine midfoot stability, and the fold-over tongue, at a more accessible price. The Elite adds a higher-spec upper and plate for the top tier. If you want most of the Predator control feel without the Elite outlay, the Pro is the pick.

### Does the Predator Pro turf run true to size?

It does for most players. For a narrow foot or a tighter, more locked-in fit, drop a half size. Wider feet fit well at true size and need little break-in.

### Can I wear these on natural grass?

You can on a kickabout, but it's not what they're for. The short-stud turf outsole is tuned for artificial and hard surfaces. For natural grass you'd want the firm-ground (FG) version, and matching the outsole to your surface protects both your footing and the shoe.
