## FAQs about the Predator Club

### Does the adidas Predator Club run true to size?

It runs true to size for most players, though it sits a touch snug. If you have a wider foot or like a little room, go up a half size. No break-in period needed either way.

### What's the difference between the Predator Club and the Elite?

The Club is the entry tier: a comfortable synthetic upper, the fold-over tongue, and a do-it-all FG-MG outsole, priced to get you playing. The Elite sits at the top of the [Predator line](https://www.prosoccer.com/collections/adidas-predator) with a premium upper and the full control tech package. If you play rec or get back out a couple times a week, the Club has everything you actually need.

### Can I wear these on turf or artificial grass?

These are firm-multi ground (FG-MG) cleats, built for natural grass and the mixed surfaces a lot of fields become midseason. For dedicated artificial turf you'd want a turf shoe instead. Matching the outsole to your surface protects both the cleat and your footing.

### Is the fold-over tongue just for looks?

It's both. The fold-over tongue is a Predator signature, and it covers the laces to give you one clean surface across the instep for striking and control. So yes, it looks the part, and it does a job.
