## Care and Maintenance

- Air-dry after every session; stuff with newspaper to pull moisture and hold shape
- Keep them away from radiators, dryers, and hot car dashboards, which warp the rubber outsole
- Knock off turf pellets and dirt with a soft brush before they go back in the bag
- Wipe the Nanostrike mesh with a damp cloth; pull the sockliner out to dry separately after a wet session, which helps with the smell

## FAQs about the Junior Predator League Turf

### What's the difference between this turf shoe and the firm-ground cleats?

This is the turf (TF) version, built with a flat rubber outsole for artificial turf and hard, dry fields. Firm-ground cleats have molded studs for natural grass. Match the shoe to where your kid actually trains: if it's a turf field most nights, this is the right pick.

### Does the Junior Predator League turf run true to size?

It runs true to size for most kids. If your player is mid-growth-spurt or between sizes, going up a half size adds a little room without making the shoe sloppy. Wide feet fit well at true size in the regular fit.

### Is this the same as the fold-over tongue version?

No. This is the standard laced version with a floating tongue, the everyday League build. The [fold-over tongue turf shoe](https://www.prosoccer.com/products/adidas-junior-predator-league-fold-over-tongue-turf-soccer-shoes-road-to-glory-pack-sp26) covers the laces for a cleaner surface on the top of the foot. Both are League tier; this standard build is the simpler, lower-price everyday option.

### Will these hold up to a full season of training?

That's what they're built for. The Nanostrike mesh upper and rubber turf outsole are made to take repeated turf sessions. Air-dry them after each use and brush off the pellets, and they'll go the distance for a kid training several times a week.
