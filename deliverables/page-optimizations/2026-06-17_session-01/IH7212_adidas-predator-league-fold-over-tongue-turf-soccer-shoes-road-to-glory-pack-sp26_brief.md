## Care and Maintenance

- Brush off turf pellets and dirt with a soft brush after every session before they pack into the studs
- Air-dry at room temperature; stuff with newspaper to hold shape and pull moisture
- Keep them away from direct heat, sun, and dryers, which break down the synthetic upper and warp the outsole
- Wipe the upper with a damp cloth; the synthetic and Nanostrike+ mesh need no conditioner

## FAQs about the Predator League Turf

### Are these turf shoes or firm-ground cleats?

These are turf (TF) shoes, built for artificial surfaces. The outsole runs many short rubber studs that grip 3G and 4G turf, small-sided cages, and hard courts. For natural grass you'd want the firm-ground (FG) version with a studded plate instead.

### How is the League different from the Elite Predator turf?

The League is the value tier: a synthetic and textile upper with Nanostrike+, Powerspine, and the Strikeframe outsole, at an everyday price. The [adidas Predator](https://www.prosoccer.com/collections/adidas-predator) line steps up from there to Pro and Elite builds with higher-end uppers and a higher price. If you play weekly on turf and want real Predator control without the flagship cost, the League is the right call.

### Does the Predator League turf run true to size?

It runs true to size for most players. If you have a narrow foot or want a snug, locked-in feel, drop a half size. Wider feet fit well at true size with no break-in needed.
