- Knock off turf pellets and dirt after every session with a soft brush
- Wipe the synthetic upper with a damp cloth; let them air-dry away from heaters and direct sun
- Stuff with paper towel to hold shape and pull moisture between sessions
- Keep them out of the washing machine and dryer, which break down the upper and warp the outsole

## FAQs about the Junior Predator League Turf

### Are these turf shoes durable enough for a season of practice?

Yes. The rubber turf outsole and Nanostrike mesh upper are built for repeat sessions on artificial grass. Brush off the pellets and air-dry them after each use and they'll hold up through a full season of weeknight practice and weekend turf games.

### What size should I order for a growing kid?

They run true to size in youth sizing. Kids' feet grow fast, so measure each season and, if your player is between sizes, go up a half size for a little room without losing the snug fit. The size chart on the page lists the youth measurements.

### Can my kid wear these on firm natural grass?

These are turf (TF) shoes, made for artificial grass and turf pitches. For firm natural grass you'd want the firm ground version with a studded plate. Matching the outsole to the surface keeps your player's footing safe and protects the shoe.

### What does the fold-over tongue do for a young player?

It covers the laces and gives one clean surface across the top of the foot, so a strike lands flush instead of catching the laces. Young players who love to shoot tend to feel the difference right away, and it carries the senior Predator look down to a junior build.
