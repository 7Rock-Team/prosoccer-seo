## FAQs about the Kids Predator Club

### Will my young child be able to put these on themselves?

These use a standard lace closure, so a four or five-year-old will likely need a hand from you the first few times. Once they're tied, the fold-over tongue stays put and the regular fit keeps the foot secure all game. If your child is just learning laces, double-knot them before kickoff and you're set.

### Will my kid outgrow these before the season ends?

Kids this age grow fast, so it's a real worry. The Club is built and priced as the get-them-playing option for exactly that reason. If your child is mid growth spurt, going up a half size buys a little runway, and the regular fit leaves room for a thicker sock.

### Can they wear these on turf or artificial grass?

These are firm-multi ground (FG-MG) cleats, made for natural grass and the softer mixed fields a lot of youth games end up on. For a dedicated indoor or turf surface, you'd want the turf or indoor version instead. Matching the outsole to the surface keeps little feet steady and the cleat lasting longer.

### Are these tough enough for how rough kids play?

They're built for it. The synthetic upper holds up to the scuffs, slides, and full-effort kicks that come with youth soccer, and the do-it-all outsole takes the everyday beating of practice and weekend games. Brush them off and air-dry them after each session and they'll go the distance.

### When do we move up from Kids to the Junior version?

The Kids sizing covers the youngest players. Once your child grows past it and wants a build with a bit more underfoot, the [Predator line](https://www.prosoccer.com/collections/adidas-predator) steps up through Junior League and beyond. The same Predator look and control feel carries the whole way up, so the move feels familiar.
