## FAQs about the Predator Elite Fold-Over Tongue AG

### What's the difference between the AG and FG version?

Only the outsole. AG runs shorter, denser studs that grip synthetic pitches; FG runs longer studs for natural grass. Match the plate to your home surface.

### Can I wear the AG cleats on natural grass?

In a pinch, but the shorter AG studs won't bite soft natural grass like firm-ground studs do. For regular grass play, go FG.

### Are the studs on the AG plate replaceable?

No, the AG studs are molded and non-removable, standard for artificial-ground cleats built for synthetic pitches.
