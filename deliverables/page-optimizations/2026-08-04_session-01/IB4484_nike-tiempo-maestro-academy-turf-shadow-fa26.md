- Wipe the FlyTouch upper with a damp cloth after play; brush off dried turf pellets
- Air-dry away from heat, radiators, and direct sun
- The upper is synthetic, so skip the leather conditioner

## FAQs about the Tiempo Maestro Academy Turf

### Can these be worn on grass or indoor courts?

They're made for artificial turf. The rubber outsole and short studs grip synthetic surfaces best, so save grass for firm-ground cleats.

### Do they work for a wider foot?

The soft FlyTouch upper molds to the foot and gives a little as it breaks in, handling a slightly wider foot better than a stiff synthetic.

### What's the difference between the turf and firm-ground version?

This turf shoe has a rubber outsole with short studs for artificial surfaces. The firm-ground version uses molded studs built for real grass.
