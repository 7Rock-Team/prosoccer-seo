  ## The adidas Predator Accuracy.1 FG by adidas

  The Predator line has belonged to the playmaker since 1994. Beckham, Zidane, and Gerrard built half their careers in earlier generations; Trent Alexander-Arnold carries the line forward today. The Predator Accuracy.1 FG is the Predator 23 generation, the 2023 reset that traded the wraparound Edge tongue for a clean fold-back lace closure and pushed the signature rubber elements into a 3D-printed grid across the strike zones. The result is the cleat for the player whose first move is the pass, the cross, or the shot, not the sprint. This is the .1, the Elite tier, the same construction the senior squad pulls on; the rest of the Predator catalog sits inside [the Predator lineup](/collections/adidas-predator) adidas built around it.

  ## Firm Ground and Where the Plate Belongs

  The .1 FG plate is built for firm ground: natural grass on a well-maintained pitch, the surface a Saturday league or a high-school varsity match runs on. Soft, wet grass after a week of rain wants SG (soft ground, sold separately when stocked). Artificial grass wants the AG version. Indoor wants IC. Older astroturf wants TF. Don't run firm-ground studs on a hard 3G turf field or you'll wear down the tips fast and lose the bite the plate gives you on real grass. The split outsole transfers power forward through the forefoot on the strike, which is what the Predator line is built around in the first place.

  ## Who the Accuracy.1 Is For

  Predator is the passer's cleat. If you're the player whose first move is the run, the X (or now F50) is your line. If you're the player who plays off the touch and sets up the team, Copa is your line. Predator sits between the two: control, accuracy, and the rubber strike zones that put spin and placement on the ball.

  The .1 tier is the senior-team build. The .2 (Pro), .3 (League), and Club tiers below all run the same Predator silhouette at lower price points with softer or synthetic uppers and simpler plates. If you're playing competitive at the high-school or club level and you want what the first XI wears, the .1 is the one. If you're a weekend rec player or just starting out, the .2 or .3 gets you the look without the elite-tier price tag.

  ## Fit and Sizing

  The Accuracy.1 FG runs to standard adidas football-boot fit. The HybridTouch upper softens with two or three sessions of break-in; the PRIMEKNIT collar locks in around the ankle without restricting the foot strike. The forefoot runs medium-narrow, in line with the rest of the Predator line. Wide-foot players who've worn a Predator before know the fit; wide-foot players new to the line should consider a half-size up, or look at the Copa line which runs wider through the forefoot. Lace closure, no extra strap or tongue cover, so the lace tension is what you set it to.
