## FAQs about the Tiempo Maestro Academy Turf

### Where can I wear the Tiempo Maestro Academy turf shoe?
On artificial turf and older astroturf. The flat rubber outsole grips the synthetic surface without the studs you'd want on natural grass, so keep your firm-ground (FG) cleats for the grass field and use these on turf.

### What's the difference between the Academy and Elite Tiempo Maestro?
Both sit in Nike's Tiempo touch-and-control line, but the Academy is the accessible entry build and the Elite is the top tier, with premium materials and a higher price. The Academy still gives you soft FlyTouch leather for real ball feel, which is the whole point of the Tiempo line.

### Does the Tiempo Maestro Academy run true to size?
Yes, true to size for most players. The soft FlyTouch leather molds to your foot, so if you're between sizes or run wide it gives a little more than a stiff synthetic would. Try them on with your match socks.

### Is real leather worth it on a turf shoe?
If you care about your first touch, yes. Soft leather wraps the ball better than hard plastic, and the FlyTouch upper stays light enough that you don't trade away comfort for feel. That touch is what the Tiempo Maestro is known for, and you get it here at an entry price.
