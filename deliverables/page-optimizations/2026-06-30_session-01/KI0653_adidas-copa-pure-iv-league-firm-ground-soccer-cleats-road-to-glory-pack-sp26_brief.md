## Care and Maintenance

- Air-dry after every match and stuff them with paper to hold their shape

## FAQs about the Copa Pure IV League

### What surface are the Copa Pure IV League FG cleats made for?

Firm ground, which means dry, natural grass and well-kept fields. The Comfort Plate studs are cut for grass. For turf, grab the TF version; for artificial grass, the AG. Grass studs wear down fast on hard surfaces.

### Is this the same shoe as the junior Copa Pure IV League?

Same model and same look, different sizing. This page is the adult cut, running M 6.5 / W 7.5 up to M 11 / W 12. The junior version comes in youth sizes for smaller feet.

### Is the Copa Pure IV League made of leather?

No. The League tier uses adidas Fusionfeel, a soft synthetic built to feel clean and supple on the ball without the break-in or upkeep of real leather. The leather sits on the Elite and Pro Copa Pure IV.

### What's the difference between the League and the Pro or Elite Copa Pure IV?

Same Copa shape and touch, different materials and price. The Pro and Elite step up to a calfskin upper and lighter plates for higher-level play. League gives a developing or rec player most of that feel and comfort at a friendlier price.
