## Fit Notes

- Regular fit, true to size for most players.
- The Fusionskin forefoot softens and gives slightly as it breaks in, so expect a closer hug after the first few wears.
- Between sizes or wider feet: go up a half size and let the upper mold to your foot.
- Sized in adult men's and women's, M 7 / W 8 through M 11 / W 12.

## Care and Maintenance

- Wipe the Fusionskin upper down with a damp cloth after matches, then let them air-dry away from radiators and direct sun. The calfskin-soft forefoot doesn't like being baked.
- Skip the washing machine. It dries out the calfskin-soft feel that makes the Copa worth buying.
- Stuff them with newspaper between matches to hold their shape and pull moisture.
- Knock dried mud off the firm-ground outsole so the studs keep their bite.
- Work a light leather conditioner into the calfskin forefoot every few weeks to keep the touch supple through the season.

## FAQs about the Copa Pure IV Pro

### How is the Copa Pure IV Pro different from the Elite?
The Pro gives you most of what the [Copa Pure IV Elite](https://www.prosoccer.com/products/adidas-copa-pure-iv-elite-firm-ground-soccer-cleats-road-to-glory-pack-sp26) does, the same Fusionskin calfskin-soft forefoot and that clean Copa touch, in a build meant for full-season, week-in, week-out play rather than the lightest-possible flagship spec. If you want the real Copa feel without the top-tier spend, the Pro is the one. (Some shoppers search it as the Copa Pure 4 Pro, it's the same cleat.)

### What surface are the Copa Pure IV Pro FG made for?
Firm ground, which means dry natural grass, the standard league and practice pitch. The FG outsole bites into solid grass for a clean push-off. If you play mostly on artificial turf, grab the [Copa Pure IV Pro Turf version](https://www.prosoccer.com/products/adidas-copa-pure-iv-pro-turf-soccer-shoes-road-to-glory-pack-sp26) instead, and skip FG on soft, muddy ground where longer studs do the job better.

### Do the Copa Pure IV Pro run true to size?
For most players, yes, true to size in a regular fit. The Fusionskin forefoot softens and gives a little as you break it in, so they hug closer over the first few wears. If you're between sizes or have a wider foot, go up a half size and let the upper mold to you.

### Is the Copa Pure IV Pro a good cleat for touch-first players?
That's exactly who it's built for. If your game is about controlling the ball, linking passes, and receiving cleanly under pressure, the calfskin-soft Fusionskin upper gives you the contact and feel to do it. The Copa has always been the touch player's cleat, and the Pro carries that for players who don't want to spend flagship money to get it.
