## FAQs about the Superfly 11 Pro

### What is the difference between the Superfly 11 Pro and the Superfly 11 Elite?

Both are high-cut Mercurial speed cleats. The Elite is the stripped-back flagship with Nike's top-end build; the Pro keeps the same speed shape and the Air Zoom forefoot unit at a lower price, making it the value pick for players who want the Mercurial feel without the flagship cost.

### Should I get the Superfly or the Vapor?

They are the two halves of the Mercurial line. The Superfly is high-cut and built for straight-line, top-end speed. The Vapor is low-cut and tuned for close control and quick changes of direction in tight space. Pick the Superfly if your game is the run in behind; pick the Vapor if it is beating players one-on-one.

### What surface are these cleats made for?

The firm ground (FG) plate is built for natural grass and dry, well-maintained surfaces. For dedicated artificial grass, look at the AG version instead, which carries a stud pattern designed for synthetic turf.

### Do the Superfly 11 Pro run true to size?

They run snug and streamlined, the standard Mercurial speed fit. Most players can order their usual size. If you have a wider foot, going up a half size will give you more room.
