## FAQs about the Copa Pure IV Pro Turf

### What surface is the Pro Turf made for?

Artificial turf, the short synthetic-grass kind, plus older astroturf and indoor turf courts. The rubber outsole spreads grip across the surface instead of digging studs into it. For natural grass, reach for a firm-ground (FG) model.

### Does the Pro Turf run true to size?

Yes for most players. The Fusionfeel 2.0 upper is supple from the first wear and settles in a session or two, so order your normal size. Wider feet can stay true; the upper gives a little.

### Is the upper real leather?

No. This turf pair uses adidas Fusionfeel 2.0, a synthetic built to feel soft on the ball with a Touchprint texture, no break-in or leather upkeep. The calfskin Copa feel lives on the firm-ground Elite and Pro.

### Copa, Predator, or F50 for the small-sided game?

Pick the Copa Pure IV Pro Turf if you win games with your touch and want the ball glued to your foot in tight spaces. Go Predator if you live for control and the strike, or F50 if raw speed is your edge. The Copa is the touch shoe of the three.
