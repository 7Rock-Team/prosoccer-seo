## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**Is the Copa Pure IV League made of leather?**
Not on the League tier. It uses adidas Fusionfeel, a synthetic built to feel leather-soft on the ball without the break-in or upkeep of real leather. The calfskin leather shows up on the Elite and Pro Copa Pure IV.

**Can my kid wear these on turf or indoor courts?**
These are firm-ground (FG) cleats, made for dry natural grass. For turf, look for the TF version; for artificial grass, the AG; for an indoor court, an IC shoe. FG studs on hard turf wear down fast.

**What's the difference between the League and the Elite or Pro Copa Pure IV?**
Same Copa shape and look, different materials and price. Elite and Pro use calfskin leather and lighter plates for high-level play. League gives a young or rec player most of that feel and comfort at a friendlier price.

**Copa or F50 for a young player?**
Pick the Copa if your player likes to slow the game down, keep the ball, and value comfort and touch. Pick the F50 if they're the quickest one on the field and live on the shoulder of the last defender. The Copa is adidas's comfort-and-touch cleat.
