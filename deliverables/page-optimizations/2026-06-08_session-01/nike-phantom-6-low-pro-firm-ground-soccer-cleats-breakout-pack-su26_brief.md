## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**What is the difference between the Phantom 6 Pro and the Elite?**
The Elite uses Nike's full Gripknit upper and sits at the top price. The Pro keeps the same Phantom shape and the same Cyclone 360 soleplate but swaps in a VNMSkin-over-Flyknit upper to bring the price down. You lose a little of the Elite's stickiest grip; you keep most of the control. For a developing or club player, the Pro is the value sweet spot.

**Does the Phantom 6 Low Pro run true to size?**
For most players, yes, true to size. The updated frame gives a rounder, more natural toe box than past Phantoms, so if you have a slightly wider forefoot you can usually stay at your normal size. If you wear thick game socks, try them on with those before deciding.

**The Low version has no collar. How does that feel?**
The Low is a standard ankle cut with no fabric collar, so nothing wraps or grips above the foot. Players who find a collar tight or who just prefer a free ankle tend to like the Low. If you want the locked-in collar feel instead, the Phantom 6 comes in a High Pro.

**These are firm-ground (FG) cleats. Where do I wear them?**
On dry, natural grass, which covers most club and school matches. They are not made for turf (look for the TF version), artificial grass (the AG plate), or an indoor court (an IC shoe). FG studs wear down fast on hard turf, so match the cleat to your surface.
