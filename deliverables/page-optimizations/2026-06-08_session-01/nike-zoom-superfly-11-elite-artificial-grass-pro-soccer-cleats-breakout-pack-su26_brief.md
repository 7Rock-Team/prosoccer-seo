## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**These are AG cleats. Can I wear them on natural grass or turf?**
They're built for artificial grass, the dense synthetic surface a lot of clubs train and play on now. You can wear them on dry natural grass in a pinch, but the AG-Pro studs are shaped to bite synthetic, so on real grass you lose a little of that grip. For natural grass, the Superfly 11 Elite FG is the match; for old-school rubber-pellet turf, look at a TF version instead.

**Does the Superfly 11 Elite AG run true to size?**
For most players, true to size. The FlyWeave Ultra upper is a snug, speed-fit cleat by design, so if you have a wider forefoot or wear thick game socks, try it on with those socks before you commit. The fitting room in Pasadena is open until 8 pm if you want to size it in person.

**Is the high collar worth it, or should I get the low-cut?**
The high collar is a feel preference, not a performance must. The Dynamic Fit cuff seals your ankle and gives you a connected, locked-in sensation through cuts, which a lot of speed players love. If you don't like anything wrapping your ankle, the low-cut Superfly gives you the same plate and upper without the collar.

**Superfly or Vapor for a winger on artificial grass?**
Both are Mercurial speed cleats, but they feel different. The Vapor 17 is more minimal and barefoot-light for instant separation. The Superfly adds the Air Zoom unit and a more structured upper, so it's a touch heavier but returns more energy on repeated sprints. If your game is sustained, repeated attacking down the flank, the Superfly is the pick.
