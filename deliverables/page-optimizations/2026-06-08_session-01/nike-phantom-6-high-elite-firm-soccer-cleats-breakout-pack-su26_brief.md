## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**Does the Phantom 6 High Elite run true to size?**
For most players, yes, true to size. The updated frame gives a roomier, more natural toe box than past Phantoms, so if you're between sizes or have a wider forefoot, you can usually stay at your normal size rather than going up. If you wear thick game socks, try them on with those.

**These are firm-ground (FG) cleats. Where can I actually wear them?**
On dry, natural grass, which covers most league and school matches. They are not made for turf (look for the TF version), for artificial grass (the AG plate), or for an indoor court (an IC shoe). FG studs wear down fast on hard turf, so match the cleat to your surface.

**What's the difference between the High Elite and the Low Elite or the Pro?**
The High and Low are the same Elite cleat with a different collar: High wraps your ankle in a Dynamic Fit collar, Low sits at a standard cut for players who don't want a collar. The Pro tier drops to a slightly different upper and plate build at a lower price. The Elite is the top tier, with the full Gripknit upper.

**Is the Phantom 6 a finishing cleat or a speed cleat?**
A control and finishing cleat. If you live on the shoulder of the last defender and win games with pure pace, the Mercurial is the speed line. The Phantom is for the player who receives, turns, and releases: the one who shapes the play and picks the corner. The Gripknit upper is built for touch and precision, not top-end speed.
