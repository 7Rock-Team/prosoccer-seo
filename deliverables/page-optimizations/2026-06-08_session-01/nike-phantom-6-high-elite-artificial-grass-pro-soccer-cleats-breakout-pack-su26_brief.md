## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**What's the difference between the AG and the FG version of the Phantom 6 High Elite?**
The plate. AG (artificial grass) has more, shorter studs made for hard synthetic pitches, so you grip without the studs digging in. FG (firm ground) is for dry natural grass and has fewer, longer studs. If your week is mostly 3G or turf-style artificial grass, get this AG version; FG studs wear down fast on synthetic and can feel grabby.

**Why pick the High collar over the Low Elite AG?**
The High has a Dynamic Fit collar that wraps and holds your ankle; the Low Elite AG sits at a standard cut with no collar. Go High if you want the locked-in, secured feel, if you're managing a previous ankle knock, or if you just play more confidently when the joint is wrapped. Go Low if you'd rather not feel anything around the ankle. Same Elite cleat underneath either way.

**Do these run true to size?**
They run about a quarter-size long, which is consistent across recent Phantom generations. Most players size down a half size. If you're between sizes or wear thick game socks, try your normal size and a half-size down before you commit.

**Elite or Pro: which AG should I get?**
The Elite is the top tier with the full Gripknit upper spread across the whole foot for the most touch and grip. The Pro drops to a slightly simpler upper and build at a lower price and still plays great. If touch and ball control are the whole point for you, the Elite is the one. If you want most of the feel for less, the Pro AG is the smart call.
