## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**The Vapor 17 is a low-cut cleat. How does the fit run?**
For most players, true to size, with a snug, sock-like AtomKnit fit through the midfoot. It's a low collar, so there's no ankle sleeve to size around; you just get a close, locked-down feel. If you have a wider forefoot or wear thick game socks, try them on with those, and if you're truly between sizes the snug knit means you can usually stay at your normal size rather than going up.

**These are firm-ground (FG) cleats. Where can I wear them?**
On dry, natural grass, which covers most league and school matches. They're not built for turf (look for the TF version), for artificial grass (the AG plate), or for an indoor court (an IC shoe). The FG studs wear down fast on hard turf, so match the cleat to your surface to keep the plate and studs intact.

**What's the difference between the Vapor 17 and the Superfly 11?**
Same Mercurial family, two different jobs as of the 2026 generation. The Vapor is the low-cut agility cleat: AtomKnit upper, FlyLite plate, built for quick cuts and winning in tight space. The Superfly is the high-collar speed cleat built for top-end, straight-line pace down the open channel. If you live on the dribble and the change of direction, the Vapor is your cleat.

**Is the Elite worth it over the Pro tier?**
The Elite is the top build, with the full AtomKnit upper and the FlyLite plate, so you get the lightest, closest-to-barefoot feel Nike makes in the Vapor. The Pro tier drops to a slightly heavier upper and plate at a lower price and still plays the same agility game. If you want the lightest touch and the sharpest cuts, the Elite is the one.
