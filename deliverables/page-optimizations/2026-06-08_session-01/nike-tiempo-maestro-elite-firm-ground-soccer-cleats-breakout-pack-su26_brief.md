## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**Does the Tiempo Maestro Elite run true to size?**
For most players, yes, true to size. The Techleather upper is soft from the box and molds to your foot quickly, so you don't need to size up for break-in the way old k-leather cleats demanded. If you're between sizes or have a wider midfoot, the glove-like wrap tends to forgive that better than a stiff synthetic. Try them on with your game socks.

**Is this real leather, and how do I break it in and care for it?**
It's Techleather, Nike's synthetic leather, not natural k-leather. That's good news for break-in: it's soft on day one and won't soak up water and go heavy like real leather does. Wipe the dirt off after matches, let them air dry away from direct heat, and never put them in a dryer. Stuff them with newspaper if they get soaked.

**These are firm-ground (FG) cleats. Where can I actually wear them?**
On dry, natural grass, which covers most league and school matches. They aren't made for turf (look for the TF version), for artificial grass (the AG plate), or for an indoor court (an IC shoe). FG studs wear down fast on hard turf, so match the cleat to your surface.

**Should I get the Tiempo Maestro or a speed cleat like the Mercurial?**
Depends on how you win games. If your value is pace and getting in behind, the Mercurial is the speed line. The Tiempo Maestro is for the player who controls the game through touch: receiving under pressure, turning, and dictating the pass. The Techleather upper and split plate are tuned for feel and control, not top-end sprint speed.
