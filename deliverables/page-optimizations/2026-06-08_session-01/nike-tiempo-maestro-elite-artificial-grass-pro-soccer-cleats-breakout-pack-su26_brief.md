## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**Are these AG cleats right for my surface, or should I get the firm-ground version?**
Get the AG version if you play most of your matches on artificial grass, the green synthetic 3G surface. The AG-Pro plate uses shorter conical studs that grip turf evenly and are warrantied for it, where firm-ground studs can catch and wear fast on synthetic. If your home pitch is natural grass, the Maestro Elite comes in a firm-ground (FG) build instead. Match the plate to the surface you actually play on most.

**Does the Tiempo Maestro Elite run true to size?**
For most players, true to size, with a snug rather than roomy fit through the midfoot. The TECHLEATHER upper molds to your foot over the first couple of sessions, so a close fit out of the box is normal and tends to settle in. If you have a wider foot, try them on with your match socks before committing, since the Maestro is built more for a locked-in feel than a generous toe box.

**TECHLEATHER on turf, how do I keep it lasting?**
TECHLEATHER is lower-maintenance than natural leather because it doesn't soak up water the way K-leather does. Knock the turf pellets off after a session, wipe the upper down, and let them air-dry away from direct heat. Don't machine-wash them and don't leave them baking in a hot car. Treated that way, the synthetic leather holds its touch and shape across a full turf season.

**Is the Maestro a control cleat or a speed cleat?**
A control and touch cleat, built for the player who receives, turns, and dictates rather than the one who wins games on pure pace. If top-end speed is your game, Nike's Mercurial line is the speed silo. The Tiempo Maestro is for the deep-lying playmaker who sets the tempo, and the TECHLEATHER upper is engineered for feel on the ball, not for shaving grams.
