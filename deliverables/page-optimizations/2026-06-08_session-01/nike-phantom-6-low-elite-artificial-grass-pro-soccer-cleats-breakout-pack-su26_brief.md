## FAQ (4 Q&As, net-new value beyond the Description)

**What is the difference between the AG and FG version of the Phantom 6 Low Elite?**
The upper is the same; the outsole is not. The AG (artificial grass) version uses the AG-Pro plate, with shorter, denser conical studs made for synthetic carpet and firmer surfaces. The FG (firm-ground) version uses longer studs made for dry natural grass. If you play most of your matches on a 3G or artificial pitch, the AG plate is the safer, longer-lasting choice.

**Is the Low collar worse for ankle support than the High?**
It is not worse, it is a different preference. The Low sits at a standard ankle height and leaves the joint free, which many players want for a quicker, lighter feel through turns and releases. The High adds a Dynamic Fit collar that wraps the ankle for a locked-in feel. Neither is a brace; pick the one that matches how you like your ankle to feel when you cut.

**Does the Phantom 6 run true to size?**
It runs about a quarter-size long, which is consistent across recent Phantom generations. Most players should try their normal size and a half-size down before committing, and lean to the half-size down if you are between sizes. The updated frame gives a roomier, more natural toe box, so wider feet are usually well served.

**Is this a finishing cleat or a speed cleat?**
A control and finishing cleat. The Gripknit upper is built for touch, receiving, and precision rather than top-end pace. If your game is pure speed off the last defender, the Mercurial is the speed line. The Phantom is for the player who receives, turns, and releases, and who wants that touch tuned for the artificial surface.
