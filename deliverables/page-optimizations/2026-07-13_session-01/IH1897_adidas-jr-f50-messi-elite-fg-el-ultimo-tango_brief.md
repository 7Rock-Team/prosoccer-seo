## Care and Maintenance
- Air-dry after every match; stuff with newspaper to hold shape and pull moisture out
- Keep them off direct heat, sunlight, and the dryer, which warp the plate and dry out the upper
- Brush off dried grass and dirt before storing
- The Hybridtouch+ synthetic upper needs only a soft brush and a damp wipe, no leather conditioner
- Store somewhere cool and dry, not a hot car or a closed bag
