Two sizes, S/M and L/XL. Check the size chart against your usual sock size. These sit on the leg rather than the foot, so your regular [soccer socks](https://www.prosoccer.com/collections/soccer-socks) still go on underneath.

## Care and Maintenance

- Machine wash cold, 86°F (30°C) or below
- Skip the fabric softener; it dulls the wicking
- Air-dry, or tumble dry low, 105°F (40°C)

## FAQs about the Nike Strike Sleeves

### Are these full socks?

No. They're footless sleeves that pull on over your shin guard, with a sock underneath.

### Do they go over or under the shin guard?

Over. The sleeve goes on top of the guard.

### Can I wear them with grip socks?

Yes. Grip sock first, guard over it, sleeve on top.
