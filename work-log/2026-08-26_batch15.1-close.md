## Registry-convention drift found while appending, NOT propagated

Batch 15 wrote two field conventions differently from every batch before it, and Batch 15.1 followed the older dominant form rather than the newer one. Flagging rather than fixing, because it needs one decision, not ten edits:

- **`url` form.** 131 of 158 pre-existing rows carry the full `https://www.prosoccer.com/products/...` URL, including all of B13 and B14. All 10 B15 rows carry the bare `/products/...` path. B15.1 used the full URL.
- **`product_type` for indoor footwear.** B14 and earlier use `shoe-indoor` (KK1061, KK1049, IH4577). B15 used `shoe` (JR8971, JH7718). B15.1 used `shoe-indoor`, and `cleat-turf` for the turf shoe, matching KK1319, KK1321 and KJ3409.

Neither affects targeting or copy. Both make the registry harder to query consistently, and a mixed `url` column will break any join that assumes one form.
